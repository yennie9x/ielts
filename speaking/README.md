## IELTS Speaking Test Format

| Part | Duration | Format | Focus |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| **Part 1** | 4-5 mins | Short Q&A | Familiar topics about yourself |
| **Part 2** | 3-4 mins | Long turn (1 min prep + 1-2 mins speak) | Describe a topic in detail |
| **Part 3** | 4-5 mins | Discussion | Abstract ideas related to Part 2 |

## Tips for Each Part

### Part 1 Tips
1. Keep answers 2-4 sentences (not too short, not too long)
2. Give reasons and examples
3. Speak naturally—don't memorize scripts
4. It's okay to pause briefly to think

### Part 2 Tips
1. Use all prompts on the cue card
2. Structure: Introduction → Details → Why it matters → Conclusion
3. Aim for 1.5-2 minutes of speaking
4. Use the 1-minute prep time to jot down keywords

### Part 3 Tips
1. Give detailed, analytical answers
2. Consider multiple perspectives
3. Use phrases like "In my opinion...", "On the other hand..."
4. Support ideas with examples
