# Part 2: Describe your favorite room

## Cue Card

**Describe your favorite room in your home.**

You should say:
- Which room it is
- What it looks like
- What you do in this room
- And explain why it is your favorite room

---

## Sample Answer

My favorite room in my apartment is definitely the living room. It's where I spend most of my time when I'm at home in Ho Chi Minh City.

The room is fairly spacious for a city apartment—probably about 20 square meters. It has a modern, minimalist design which I really like. The walls are painted in neutral colors—light gray and white—which creates a calm atmosphere. There's a comfortable L-shaped sofa that faces a large TV mounted on the wall. I also have a small bookshelf in the corner with some books and decorations. The lighting is adjustable—I can switch between bright light for when I'm active and dim light for relaxing in the evening.

I do many things in this room. After work, it's where I unwind—I sit on the sofa and watch movies or TV series. I also play video games there, using my PlayStation connected to the big TV. Sometimes I read on the sofa, especially on lazy weekend mornings. When friends come over, we hang out in the living room, chatting, having snacks, or watching something together.

The reason it's my favorite room is that it's my personal sanctuary. After a long day of interviewing candidates and dealing with work stress, the living room is where I decompress. The comfortable sofa, the entertainment options, the calm colors—everything is designed for relaxation. It's also the most versatile room in my apartment; it serves multiple purposes depending on my mood.

Living alone means I have complete control over this space, and I've arranged it exactly how I like it. It reflects my personality—simple, functional, and comfortable. That's why I always look forward to coming home and spending time there.
