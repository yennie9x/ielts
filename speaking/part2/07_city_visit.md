# Part 2: Describe a city you would like to visit

## Cue Card

**Describe a city you would like to visit in the future.**

You should say:
- What city it is
- Where it is located
- What you know about it
- And explain why you want to visit this city

---

## Sample Answer

A city I've always wanted to visit is Tokyo, Japan. It's been on my bucket list for years, and I hope to go there someday soon.

Tokyo is the capital of Japan, located on the eastern coast of Honshu, the main island. It's one of the largest and most populous cities in the world, with over 13 million people in the city proper and even more in the greater metropolitan area.

I know quite a bit about Tokyo from movies, documentaries, and articles I've read. It's famous for being an incredible blend of ultra-modern and traditional. On one hand, you have districts like Shibuya and Shinjuku with their neon lights, skyscrapers, and cutting-edge technology. On the other hand, you have ancient temples, traditional gardens, and historic neighborhoods like Asakusa. The food scene is legendary—Tokyo has more Michelin-starred restaurants than any other city. And of course, it's a paradise for technology and gaming enthusiasts like me, with areas like Akihabara full of electronics and gaming shops.

There are several reasons why I want to visit Tokyo. First, as someone who works in tech recruitment, I'm fascinated by Japan's technology industry and work culture. I'd love to experience it firsthand. Second, I'm a huge fan of Japanese cuisine—sushi, ramen, wagyu beef—and eating authentic Japanese food in Tokyo would be amazing. Third, the contrast between modern and traditional aspects of the city appeals to my curiosity. I want to see cherry blossoms in spring, visit ancient shrines, and then explore futuristic areas.

I think Tokyo would be an unforgettable experience, and it's definitely the next international destination I want to tick off my list.
