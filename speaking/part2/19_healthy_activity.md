# Part 2: Describe an activity you do to stay healthy

## Cue Card

**Describe an activity you do to stay healthy.**

You should say:
- What the activity is
- How often you do it
- Where you do it
- And explain how this activity helps you stay healthy

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about going to the gym, which is my main activity for staying healthy and fit.

I try to go to the gym about three to four times a week. Usually, I go after work in the evenings, around 6 or 7 PM. Sometimes, if my schedule allows, I'll go on weekend mornings as well. I've maintained this routine fairly consistently for the past two years.

The gym I go to is located near my apartment in Ho Chi Minh City. It's a well-equipped fitness center with cardio machines, weight training equipment, and a small area for stretching and floor exercises. The convenience of its location makes it easier for me to stick to my routine—if it were far away, I'd probably find excuses to skip sessions.

My typical workout includes a mix of activities. I usually start with cardio—either running on the treadmill or using the elliptical machine for about 20 minutes to warm up. Then I focus on weight training, targeting different muscle groups on different days. I end with stretching to prevent muscle soreness.

This activity helps me stay healthy in multiple ways. Physically, regular exercise improves my cardiovascular health, builds strength, and helps me maintain a healthy weight. As someone with a sedentary job—I sit at a desk most of the day recruiting—this physical activity is essential to counterbalance all that sitting.

Beyond the physical benefits, going to the gym significantly improves my mental health. Exercise releases endorphins, which boost my mood and reduce stress. After a challenging day at work, an hour at the gym helps me clear my mind and release tension. I always leave feeling better than when I arrived. It's become an important part of maintaining both my physical and mental well-being.
