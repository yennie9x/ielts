# Part 2: Describe your job or career

## Cue Card

**Describe your job or career.**

You should say:
- What your job is
- What you do in your job
- How long you have been doing it
- And explain what you like or dislike about your job

---

## Sample Answer

I work as an IT recruiter in Ho Chi Minh City, specializing in finding and placing candidates for programming and technology positions at various companies.

My job involves several key responsibilities. First, I source candidates—this means searching for potential hires through job portals, LinkedIn, and professional networks. When I find promising candidates, I screen their resumes and conduct initial interviews to assess their skills and fit. Then I coordinate with hiring managers at client companies to present suitable candidates and arrange further interviews. I also handle negotiations when offers are made, helping both parties reach agreements on salary and terms. Beyond individual placements, I maintain relationships with both clients and candidates, building a network that helps with future positions.

I've been working in this field for about four years now. I started shortly after graduating from university, when I moved from my hometown Chau Doc to Ho Chi Minh City to pursue this career. Over time, I've specialized more in the tech sector because I find it fascinating and because there's high demand for IT talent.

What I like most about my job is the impact I can have on people's lives. When I successfully place someone in their dream job, it's incredibly rewarding. I also enjoy the variety—no two days are exactly the same, and I'm constantly learning about new technologies and companies. The tech industry is dynamic, which keeps things interesting.

What I find challenging is the pressure to meet targets and the occasional frustration when placements fall through at the last minute—perhaps a candidate accepts another offer or a company changes its requirements. It can also be stressful managing multiple positions and candidates simultaneously.

Overall, though, I enjoy my career. It suits my personality—I'm organized, good with people, and genuinely interested in technology.
