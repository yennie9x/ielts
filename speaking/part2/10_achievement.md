# Part 2: Describe an achievement you're proud of

## Cue Card

**Describe an achievement you are proud of.**

You should say:
- What you achieved
- When this happened
- How you achieved it
- And explain why you are proud of this achievement

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about landing my first job as an IT recruiter in Ho Chi Minh City. It's an achievement that marked a turning point in my life.

This happened about four years ago, shortly after I graduated from university. I had moved to Ho Chi Minh City from my hometown Chau Doc with the goal of building a career, but I had no professional experience and limited connections in the city. The job market was competitive, and I faced many rejections before finally getting this opportunity.

Achieving this required persistence and continuous self-improvement. I sent out dozens of applications and went through numerous interviews. After each rejection, I analyzed what went wrong and worked on improving—whether it was my interview skills, my knowledge of the recruitment industry, or my English proficiency. I also spent time learning about the tech industry, since the role required understanding technical positions and communicating with IT professionals.

The interview process for my first job was rigorous—three rounds over two weeks. I prepared extensively, researching the company and practicing common interview questions. When I finally received the job offer, I was overwhelmed with emotion. I called my mother in Chau Doc immediately to share the news.

I'm proud of this achievement for several reasons. First, it validated that my decision to move to the city was right—I had taken a risk, and it paid off. Second, it proved to myself that I could succeed on my own merit, away from the familiar environment of my hometown. Third, this job opened doors to my current career; everything I've built professionally stems from that first opportunity.

Looking back, that moment taught me the value of resilience and not giving up. It's an achievement I carry with me as motivation whenever I face new challenges.
