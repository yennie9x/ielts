# Part 2: Describe a place you like to visit

## Cue Card

**Describe a place you like to visit in your free time.**

You should say:
- Where this place is
- How often you go there
- What you do there
- And explain why you like this place

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my hometown Chau Doc, which is my favorite place to visit whenever I have time off from work.

Chau Doc is a small town in the Mekong Delta region, near the Cambodian border. It's about five to six hours from Ho Chi Minh City by car. I try to visit at least two or three times a year, usually during Tet and other major holidays, and occasionally for long weekends when I can manage the time off.

When I'm there, I do several things. First and foremost, I spend quality time with my family—eating home-cooked meals, catching up on everyone's lives, and just enjoying being together. I also love revisiting places from my childhood. I often go to Sam Mountain, which has beautiful temples and offers stunning views of the surrounding countryside. The Tra Su Melaleuca Forest is another spot I enjoy—you can take a boat through the flooded forest and see incredible birdlife. And of course, I never miss the chance to eat local specialties like bún cá and grilled fish, which Chau Doc is famous for.

The reason I love visiting Chau Doc is that it feels like a complete escape from city life. Ho Chi Minh City is exciting but exhausting—the traffic, the noise, the constant hustle. In Chau Doc, everything moves at a slower pace. The air is fresher, the people are friendlier, and life feels simpler. It's also deeply personal because it's where I grew up and where my roots are.

Every time I leave Chau Doc to return to the city, I feel recharged and reconnected to what really matters—family, nature, and a sense of peace. That's why it will always be my favorite place to visit.
