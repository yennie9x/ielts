# Part 2: Describe a piece of technology you use

## Cue Card

**Describe a piece of technology that you find useful.**

You should say:
- What it is
- How often you use it
- How you learned to use it
- And explain why you find it useful

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my laptop, which is probably the most essential piece of technology I own besides my smartphone.

It's a Windows laptop that I use primarily for work. It's a mid-range model—nothing too fancy, but reliable and fast enough for my needs as an IT recruiter. The screen is large enough to comfortably review resumes and have video calls, and the battery life is decent for when I need to work outside the office.

I use my laptop almost every day, typically for several hours. At work, it's indispensable—I use it for accessing our recruitment platform, managing candidate databases, writing emails, preparing reports, and conducting video interviews. At home, I sometimes use it for personal tasks like browsing, researching, or occasionally working remotely when needed.

Learning to use computers was a gradual process. I started using computers in school, learning basic programs like Microsoft Word and Excel. Over time, as technology evolved and I entered the workforce, I picked up new skills as needed—learning recruitment software, video conferencing tools, and various productivity applications. Most of my learning has been self-directed, through trial and error or online tutorials.

The reason I find my laptop so useful is that it enables my entire career. Without it, I simply couldn't do my job effectively. Everything from finding candidates on LinkedIn to communicating with clients to tracking hiring pipelines happens on my laptop. It's also my gateway to staying informed about tech industry trends, which is crucial for recruiting in the IT sector.

Beyond work, it gives me flexibility. If I need to work from home or catch up on tasks during evenings, my laptop makes that possible. In today's digital age, having a reliable computer isn't a luxury—it's a necessity for most professional work.
