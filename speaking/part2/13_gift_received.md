# Part 2: Describe a gift you received

## Cue Card

**Describe a gift you received that was important to you.**

You should say:
- What the gift was
- Who gave it to you
- When you received it
- And explain why this gift was important to you

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about a pair of high-quality headphones that my close friends gave me for my birthday last year. It's one of the most thoughtful gifts I've ever received.

The headphones are wireless, noise-canceling ones from Sony—the WH-1000XM4 model, which is known for its excellent sound quality and comfort. They're black with a sleek, minimalist design, which suits my taste perfectly.

My friends—a group of four people I've known since university—gave me this gift on my birthday last year. We were having dinner at a Vietnamese restaurant in Ho Chi Minh City when they surprised me with the gift. I had no idea they were planning anything, so it was completely unexpected.

What made this gift so special was how much thought went into it. My friends knew that I use headphones constantly—for music during my commute, for gaming in the evenings, for work calls, and for watching movies. They had noticed that my old headphones were getting worn out but knew I wouldn't buy new ones for myself because I tend to prioritize other expenses. So they pooled their money together to get me something I actually needed but wouldn't splurge on.

The gift is important to me not just because of its practical value, but because it represents our friendship. It showed that my friends pay attention to my life and care about making me happy. Every time I use the headphones—which is daily—I'm reminded of their thoughtfulness and our bond.

It also taught me something about giving gifts: the best presents aren't necessarily the most expensive ones, but the ones that show you truly understand the person. I try to apply this lesson when choosing gifts for others now.
