# Part 2: Describe a successful person you know

## Cue Card

**Describe a successful person you know or have heard of.**

You should say:
- Who this person is
- What they do
- How they became successful
- And explain why you consider them successful

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about a senior manager at a tech company I work with as a recruiter. His name is Mr. Tuan, and he's one of the most successful professionals I've encountered in my career.

Mr. Tuan is the Head of Engineering at a well-known software company in Ho Chi Minh City. He oversees multiple development teams and is responsible for some of the company's most important products. I first met him when I was recruiting developers for his team, and I was immediately impressed by his leadership style.

What's remarkable about his journey is that he started from humble beginnings. He grew up in a rural area, similar to my hometown Chau Doc, and was the first in his family to attend university. He taught himself programming through online resources and books because his university didn't have advanced courses. After graduating, he worked his way up from a junior developer, learning constantly and taking on challenging projects that others avoided.

He became successful through a combination of hard work, continuous learning, and excellent people skills. Despite being technically brilliant, he never looks down on others. He mentors junior developers and creates a positive work environment. That's why his teams are so productive—people genuinely want to work for him.

I consider him successful not just because of his title or salary, but because he's achieved professional excellence while remaining a good human being. He balances work with family life, he's respected by his peers, and he gives back by mentoring others. To me, that's true success—achieving your goals without compromising your values. He's someone I look up to in my own career journey.
