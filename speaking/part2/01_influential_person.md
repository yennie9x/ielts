# Part 2: Describe a person who has influenced you

## Cue Card

**Describe a person who has had a great influence on your life.**

You should say:
- Who this person is
- How you know this person
- What this person has done to influence you
- And explain why this person has had such an influence on you

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my mother, who has had the greatest influence on my life. She's the person I admire most in the world.

My mother raised me and my siblings in Chau Doc, a small town in the Mekong Delta near Cambodia. She worked incredibly hard to provide for our family, often sacrificing her own comfort to make sure we had everything we needed for school and daily life.

What influenced me most was her work ethic and resilience. Despite facing many challenges, she never complained and always remained positive. She taught me the value of perseverance—that if you work hard and stay patient, you can overcome any obstacle. When I was considering moving to Ho Chi Minh City for better career opportunities, she encouraged me to take the risk, even though it meant being far from home.

She also instilled in me the importance of education. She always told me that knowledge is something no one can take away from you. That's why I took my studies seriously and eventually became an IT recruiter, a career that allows me to help others find opportunities just as she helped me find mine.

The reason she's had such an impact is that her actions spoke louder than words. She didn't just tell me how to live—she showed me through her example. Her strength, kindness, and unwavering support have shaped who I am today. Even now, living in Ho Chi Minh City, I call her regularly and visit Chau Doc whenever I can. She's my biggest inspiration, and I hope I can make her proud with everything I achieve.
