# Part 2: Describe a hobby you enjoy

## Cue Card

**Describe a hobby you enjoy doing.**

You should say:
- What the hobby is
- How long you have been doing it
- How often you do it
- And explain why you enjoy this hobby

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about playing video games, which has been one of my favorite hobbies for many years.

I've been gaming since I was about eight or nine years old. It started when I would go to internet cafes in my hometown Chau Doc to play games with friends after school. That was over fifteen years ago, and gaming has remained a constant hobby throughout my life, evolving from simple games to more complex ones as I've grown older.

Currently, I play video games several times a week—usually in the evenings after work and more extensively on weekends. During weekdays, I might play for an hour or so to unwind after a long day of recruiting. On weekends, if I have no other plans, I can spend several hours gaming, especially if I'm playing with friends online.

The types of games I enjoy have changed over time. These days, I mostly play strategy games like Age of Empires and multiplayer online games where I can team up with friends. I appreciate games that challenge me mentally and require planning and coordination.

There are several reasons why I enjoy gaming. First, it's an excellent stress reliever. After a demanding day at work, gaming allows me to completely disconnect and focus on something fun. Second, it's social—I often play with friends, and it's a way to stay connected with people even when we can't meet in person. Third, I genuinely find it intellectually stimulating; strategy games in particular require critical thinking and quick decision-making.

Some people might view gaming as a waste of time, but I see it as a legitimate form of entertainment, no different from watching movies or sports. As long as it's balanced with other responsibilities—which I try to ensure—it's a hobby that brings me genuine joy.
