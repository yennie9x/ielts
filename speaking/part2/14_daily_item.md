# Part 2: Describe something you use every day

## Cue Card

**Describe something you use every day.**

You should say:
- What it is
- How long you have had it
- What you use it for
- And explain why it is important to you

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my smartphone, which I use constantly throughout every single day. I honestly can't imagine life without it.

My phone is a mid-range Android device—I've had this particular model for about two years now. Before this, I used an older phone, but I upgraded when my work demands increased and I needed something more reliable.

I use my phone for countless things. For work, it's essential—I check emails, browse LinkedIn for candidates, make calls to clients and job seekers, and use messaging apps like Zalo to communicate. As an IT recruiter, being reachable and responsive is crucial, and my phone makes that possible. Beyond work, I use it for entertainment: streaming music on Spotify during my motorbike commute, watching YouTube videos, playing mobile games during breaks, and scrolling through social media. I also use it for practical daily tasks like navigation, checking the weather, setting alarms, and mobile banking.

The reason my phone is so important is that it's essentially my connection to everything—work, friends, family, information, entertainment. It's how I stay in touch with my mother in Chau Doc through video calls. It's how I coordinate meetups with friends. It's how I stay informed about tech industry news, which is vital for my job.

If I forgot my phone at home, I'd feel genuinely anxious. That might sound like an exaggeration, but in today's world, smartphones have become extensions of ourselves. Mine keeps me productive, connected, and entertained. While I try to be mindful about screen time and not let it consume my life entirely, I have to admit that it's the single most important object I own in terms of daily utility.
