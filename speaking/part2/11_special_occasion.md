# Part 2: Describe a special occasion or celebration

## Cue Card

**Describe a special occasion or celebration you attended.**

You should say:
- What the occasion was
- When and where it took place
- Who was there
- And explain why it was special to you

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about last year's Tet celebration with my family in Chau Doc. It was particularly special because it was the first time in two years that our whole family could be together.

Tet, or Vietnamese Lunar New Year, is the most important holiday in Vietnam. It took place in late January last year. I traveled from Ho Chi Minh City back to my hometown Chau Doc, which is about five to six hours by car. The celebration lasted for several days, as is traditional.

My entire immediate family was there—my parents, my sibling, and relatives from my extended family including grandparents, aunts, uncles, and cousins. The house was full of people, laughter, and the delicious smell of traditional Tet dishes being prepared. We had bánh tét, thịt kho hột vịt, and many other traditional foods that my mother and aunts spent days making.

During the celebration, we did many traditional activities. On New Year's Eve, we gathered to count down and wish each other a prosperous new year. We visited the local temple to pray for good fortune. Children received lucky money in red envelopes, and we spent time visiting relatives and neighbors to exchange good wishes. In the evenings, we sat around talking, playing cards, and watching Tet TV programs together.

This occasion was special for several reasons. The previous two years, various circumstances had prevented us from gathering fully—work commitments, travel restrictions, and so on. So being together again felt precious. I realized how much I had missed these moments—the warmth of family, the traditions, the sense of belonging. Living alone in Ho Chi Minh City, it's easy to feel disconnected from home. This Tet reminded me of my roots and recharged me emotionally. I returned to the city feeling grateful and motivated.
