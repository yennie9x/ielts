# Part 2: Describe a book or movie you enjoyed

## Cue Card

**Describe a book or movie you have enjoyed recently.**

You should say:
- What it was
- What it was about
- When you read/watched it
- And explain why you enjoyed it

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about the movie "Inception" directed by Christopher Nolan. Even though it's not a recent release—it came out in 2010—I rewatched it a few months ago and was reminded why it's one of my all-time favorites.

The movie is a science fiction thriller about a skilled thief named Dom Cobb, played by Leonardo DiCaprio, who specializes in extracting valuable secrets from people's dreams. He's offered a chance to have his criminal record erased if he can successfully perform "inception"—planting an idea in someone's mind rather than stealing one. The plot involves navigating multiple layers of dreams, each with different rules and time flows, which creates incredibly complex and visually stunning sequences.

I first watched Inception years ago, but I rewatched it on a weekend evening at home when I was looking for something engaging to watch. I ended up watching it twice in one month because there's so much detail to catch.

I enjoyed this movie for several reasons. First, the concept is brilliantly original—the idea of dream heists and nested dream levels is fascinating and makes you question the nature of reality. Second, the execution is masterful; the visuals are breathtaking, especially the famous scene where Paris folds onto itself. Third, the movie is intellectually stimulating—it challenges you to keep track of multiple storylines happening simultaneously across different dream layers.

As someone who works in a field that requires analytical thinking, I appreciate movies that make me think rather than just passively watch. Inception delivers on both entertainment and intellectual engagement. Every time I watch it, I notice something new, which is the mark of a truly great film. It's the kind of movie I recommend to everyone.
