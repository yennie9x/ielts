# Part 2: Describe a memorable trip

## Cue Card

**Describe a memorable trip you have taken.**

You should say:
- Where you went
- Who you went with
- What you did there
- And explain why this trip was memorable

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about a trip I took to Da Lat with my friends about two years ago. It remains one of the most memorable trips I've ever had.

Da Lat is a highland city in central Vietnam, famous for its cool climate, pine forests, and beautiful landscapes. It's often called the "City of Eternal Spring" because of its pleasant weather year-round. I went there with four close friends from university—we'd been planning the trip for months and finally found a long weekend when everyone was free.

During our three-day trip, we did a lot of activities. We visited the famous landmarks like the Crazy House, Xuan Huong Lake, and the Valley of Love. We also explored some lesser-known spots that locals recommended, like hidden waterfalls and coffee plantations. One highlight was renting motorbikes and riding through the scenic mountain roads surrounded by pine trees—the fresh, cool air was such a contrast to the heat of Ho Chi Minh City. In the evenings, we tried local specialties like bánh tráng nướng and enjoyed the night market.

What made this trip so memorable was the combination of beautiful scenery and quality time with friends. We hadn't all been together like that since graduating university, and it felt like reliving our student days—staying up late talking, laughing at inside jokes, and just enjoying each other's company without the pressures of work.

Another reason it stands out is that it was my first proper vacation after starting my career as an IT recruiter. Work had been intense, and this trip reminded me how important it is to take breaks and maintain friendships. The photos and memories from that trip still make me smile. We've already promised to do another reunion trip, hopefully soon.
