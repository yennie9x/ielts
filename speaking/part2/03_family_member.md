# Part 2: Describe a family member you spend time with

## Cue Card

**Describe a family member you like to spend time with.**

You should say:
- Who this person is
- How often you spend time with them
- What you do together
- And explain why you enjoy spending time with them

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my younger sibling, who I really enjoy spending time with whenever I visit my hometown Chau Doc.

Since I moved to Ho Chi Minh City for work, I don't see my family as often as I'd like. I typically visit during major holidays like Tet, and sometimes for long weekends—maybe three or four times a year. It's about a five to six hour drive, so I can't go back frequently. But when I do, spending time with my sibling is always a highlight.

When we're together, we do various things. We often stay up late talking about life—work, relationships, dreams for the future. We also enjoy playing video games together, which is something we've done since childhood. Sometimes we go out to explore Chau Doc, visiting local cafes or eating at our favorite street food spots. During Tet, we help our parents prepare traditional dishes and visit relatives together.

What I enjoy most about spending time with them is how comfortable and natural it feels. There's no pretense—we can be completely ourselves around each other. Even though we have different personalities in some ways, we understand each other deeply. We share the same sense of humor, the same childhood memories, and the same values our parents instilled in us.

Another reason I treasure this time is that it reminds me of where I come from. Living in a big city, it's easy to get caught up in the fast pace of life. Being with my sibling in Chau Doc brings me back to my roots and helps me stay grounded. It's a precious connection that I make sure to maintain despite the distance.
