# Part 2: Describe a skill you want to learn

## Cue Card

**Describe a skill you would like to learn.**

You should say:
- What the skill is
- Why you want to learn it
- How you would learn it
- And explain how this skill would benefit you

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about learning to play the guitar, which is a skill I've wanted to develop for many years but haven't yet pursued seriously.

The guitar has always fascinated me. I love music and listen to it daily, but I've never learned to play an instrument properly. When I was younger, I tried briefly but gave up because I didn't have the patience or discipline to practice regularly. Now, as an adult, I feel more motivated and mature enough to commit to learning.

The main reason I want to learn guitar is personal fulfillment. There's something magical about being able to create music yourself rather than just consuming it. I imagine being able to play my favorite songs, perhaps even write my own music someday. It would add a new dimension to my appreciation of music.

If I were to learn, I'd probably use a combination of methods. I'd start with online tutorials on YouTube—there are countless free resources for beginners. Then, if I became serious about it, I might take lessons from a professional teacher to correct any bad habits and accelerate my progress. I'd also need to practice consistently, perhaps 20-30 minutes daily, which would require discipline.

This skill would benefit me in several ways. First, it would be a creative outlet—something different from my analytical work as a recruiter. Second, it's a great way to relax and de-stress; I've heard that playing music is very meditative. Third, it's a social skill—being able to play guitar at gatherings or with friends would be enjoyable. Finally, learning something challenging as an adult would prove to myself that I can still acquire new abilities outside my comfort zone.

I hope to finally make time for this in the coming year. It's been on my wish list for too long.
