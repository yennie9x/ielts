# Part 2: Describe a friend you admire

## Cue Card

**Describe a friend you admire.**

You should say:
- Who this friend is
- How long you have known them
- What qualities they have
- And explain why you admire them

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my close friend Minh, whom I've known since our university days in Ho Chi Minh City. We've been friends for about eight years now, and he's someone I truly admire.

I met Minh when we were both studying at university. We were in the same class and often worked on group projects together. We discovered we shared similar interests—both of us are into technology, gaming, and trying out new restaurants in the city. Over the years, our friendship has grown stronger, even as our careers took different paths.

What I admire most about Minh is his determination and positive attitude. He works as a software developer, and I've seen him face many challenges in his career—tight deadlines, difficult projects, failed interviews early on. But he never gives up. He has this incredible ability to stay calm under pressure and find solutions to problems. He's also very generous with his time; whenever I need advice about tech-related things for my recruitment work, he's always willing to help.

Another quality I admire is his loyalty. He's the kind of friend who will drop everything if you need him. When I first moved to Ho Chi Minh City from Chau Doc, he helped me adjust to city life and introduced me to new people. He made the transition much easier.

The main reason I admire him is that he's achieved success without changing who he is. He's still humble, kind, and genuine despite doing well in his career. He reminds me that you can be successful and still be a good person. That's the kind of friend everyone deserves to have.
