# Part 2: Describe a goal you want to achieve

## Cue Card

**Describe a goal you want to achieve in the future.**

You should say:
- What the goal is
- Why you want to achieve it
- What you need to do to achieve it
- And explain how you will feel when you achieve it

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my goal of buying my own apartment in Ho Chi Minh City, which is something I've been working toward for several years.

Currently, I rent an apartment, which is fine for now but doesn't feel permanent. Owning my own place would mean having something that's truly mine—a stable foundation and an investment in my future. Property ownership is also an important milestone in Vietnamese culture; it represents success and security.

There are several reasons why this goal matters to me. First, paying rent feels like money disappearing each month with nothing to show for it, whereas mortgage payments would build equity. Second, I want the freedom to customize my living space without landlord restrictions. Third, as I think about the future—possibly starting a family someday—having my own home feels like an important step. Finally, I'd like to show my parents, who worked hard to raise me in Chau Doc, that their sacrifices have led to tangible success.

Achieving this goal requires discipline and planning. I need to continue building my savings consistently, which means being careful about spending and perhaps taking on additional work or seeking promotions to increase my income. I also need to research the property market, understand mortgage options, and find the right location that balances affordability with convenience.

When I finally achieve this goal, I imagine feeling an immense sense of accomplishment and security. It would validate years of hard work since moving to the city from Chau Doc. I'd probably feel emotional—finally having a permanent home in the place where I've built my career. I might even invite my family from Chau Doc to visit and see what their support has helped me achieve. It would be a proud moment, not just for me, but for everyone who believed in me.
