# Part 2: Describe a quiet place you like

## Cue Card

**Describe a quiet place you like to go to.**

You should say:
- Where this place is
- How you found out about it
- How often you go there
- And explain why you like this quiet place

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to describe a quiet café that I often visit near my apartment in Ho Chi Minh City. It's become my go-to spot when I need some peace away from the city noise.

The café is located on a small side street in District 3, tucked away from the main roads. It's a small, two-story place with a garden area in the back. The interior is decorated with lots of plants, wooden furniture, and soft lighting. They play gentle background music—usually jazz or acoustic—which adds to the calm atmosphere.

I discovered this café about a year ago, completely by accident. I was walking around the neighborhood looking for a place to work remotely one afternoon, and I stumbled upon it. The moment I walked in, I knew I'd found something special. It was so different from the typical busy coffee shops in Ho Chi Minh City.

I try to visit at least once or twice a week, usually on weekend mornings or sometimes after work when I need to decompress. I go there to read, catch up on articles about tech industry trends, or just sit with my thoughts and a good cup of coffee.

The reason I love this place is that it offers a rare escape from the chaos of city life. Ho Chi Minh City can be overwhelming—the traffic, the honking, the crowds. This café feels like a hidden oasis. The quiet environment helps me think clearly, and I often come up with my best ideas there. It's also where I go when I'm feeling stressed from work. Just an hour there can completely reset my mood. I've come to think of it as my second living room—a place where I can be alone with my thoughts while still being out in the world.
