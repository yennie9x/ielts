# Part 2: Describe a decision you made

## Cue Card

**Describe an important decision you made.**

You should say:
- What the decision was
- When you made it
- How you made the decision
- And explain why it was an important decision

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about my decision to move from my hometown Chau Doc to Ho Chi Minh City to pursue my career. It was one of the most significant decisions I've ever made.

I made this decision about four years ago, shortly after graduating from university. At that time, I was at a crossroads—I could stay in or near Chau Doc, where life was familiar and comfortable, or move to Ho Chi Minh City, where the job opportunities were much greater but everything would be new and challenging.

Making this decision wasn't easy. I spent weeks weighing the pros and cons. On one hand, staying home meant being close to family, lower living costs, and the comfort of a familiar environment. On the other hand, moving to Ho Chi Minh City offered better career prospects, especially in the tech recruitment field I wanted to enter. I talked to my parents, who encouraged me to take the opportunity even though they'd miss me. I also spoke with friends who had already moved to the city to understand what to expect.

Ultimately, I decided that I was young enough to take risks and that I'd regret not trying. I reasoned that I could always return home if things didn't work out, but I might never forgive myself if I didn't at least attempt to build a career in the city.

This decision was important because it shaped my entire adult life. Everything I have now—my career as an IT recruiter, my professional network, my experiences—stems from that choice to leave home. It taught me that growth often requires stepping outside your comfort zone. The transition was difficult at first, but looking back, it was absolutely the right decision. It set me on a path that has led to personal and professional growth I couldn't have achieved otherwise.
