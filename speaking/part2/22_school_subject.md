# Part 2: Describe a subject you enjoyed at school

## Cue Card

**Describe a subject you enjoyed studying at school.**

You should say:
- What the subject was
- When you studied it
- What you learned
- And explain why you enjoyed this subject

---

## Sample Answer

I'd like to talk about English, which was my favorite subject throughout my school years and remains relevant to my life today.

I studied English from primary school all the way through university. In Vietnam, English is a compulsory subject, so I had lessons almost every year of my education. However, my interest in it went beyond just passing exams—I genuinely wanted to become proficient because I saw it as a gateway to opportunities.

In my English classes, I learned the fundamentals first—vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation. As I progressed, the focus shifted to more practical skills like reading comprehension, essay writing, and conversation. In university, I took more advanced courses that included business English and academic writing, which have proven useful in my career.

There are several reasons why I enjoyed this subject. First, I was curious about the world beyond Vietnam, and English was the key to accessing that world—through movies, music, books, and later, the internet. I remember the excitement of understanding song lyrics or movie dialogues without subtitles for the first time. Second, I had some excellent teachers who made learning engaging and encouraged us to practice speaking, not just memorizing rules. Third, I could see the practical applications. Even as a student, I knew that English would open doors professionally.

My enjoyment of English has paid off in my career as an IT recruiter. I regularly communicate with international clients and candidates, read tech articles in English, and use English-based platforms like LinkedIn. The subject I once studied for interest has become an essential professional tool. Looking back, I'm grateful that I invested time in it and didn't treat it as just another exam subject.
