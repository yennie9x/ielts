Grammar Note: morning / afternoon / evening and "the"

Not always with "the".

Common use with "the":
- in the morning
- in the afternoon
- in the evening

No "the" with specific time words:
- this morning
- yesterday afternoon
- tomorrow evening

Special cases:
- at noon (no "the")
- at night (usually no "the")
- in the night (used in specific contexts, e.g., I woke up in the night)

IELTS Speaking-friendly examples:
- I enjoy the evening most because I can relax after work.
- I enjoy evenings the most because I can relax after work.

Grammar Note: affect vs effect

Quick rule:
- affect = usually a verb (action): influence/change something
- effect = usually a noun (result): the outcome

Examples:
- Weather can affect my mood.
- The weather has a strong effect on my mood.

Easy memory tip:
- A in affect = Action (verb)
- E in effect = End result (noun)

Tenses for IELTS Speaking Part 1 (Band 6-7)

1) Present Simple (most common)
- Use: habits, facts, routines
- Example: I usually study in the evening.

2) Present Continuous
- Use: things happening around now / temporary situation
- Example: I am trying to reduce my screen time these days.

3) Present Perfect
- Use: life experience, recent change
- Example: I have used this app for about two years.

4) Past Simple
- Use: finished actions in the past
- Example: I moved to this apartment last year.

5) Future (will / be going to / would like to)
- Use: plans, intentions, preferences
- Example: I would like to travel more next year.

Tip for Band 6-7:
- Use 2-3 tenses naturally in one topic (present + past + future).
- Give one short reason in each answer (because/since/so).
