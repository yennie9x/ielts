Cambridge IELTS 14, Academic, Speaking Part 1 (Test 1-4)

Test 1 - Future
1. What job would you like to have ten years from now? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** I would like to be a senior software engineer or a team lead, mainly because I enjoy solving technical problems and mentoring junior developers.
2. How useful will English be for your future? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** It will be extremely useful since my company works with international clients, and most documentation and meetings are in English.
3. How much traveling do you hope to do in the future? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** I hope to travel two or three times a year if I can afford it, because I want to explore new cultures and take a proper break from work.
4. How do you think your life will change in the future? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** I think my life will become busier but more stable, mainly because I plan to focus on my career and possibly start a family in the next few years.
   - **Note:** After *hope*, use the **to-infinitive**: *hope to travel* (not *hope traveling*).

Test 2 - Neighbors
1. How often do you see your neighbors? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** Not very often, maybe once or twice a week in the elevator, because everyone is busy with work and we do not really hang out together.
2. Do you invite your neighbors to your home? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** Rarely, to be honest. I am quite private, and I usually only invite close friends or relatives, not people I barely know.
3. Do you think you are a good neighbor? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** I think I am a decent neighbor. I try to keep the noise down and follow the building rules, even though I do not socialize much.
4. Has a neighbor ever helped you? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** Yes, once when I forgot my keys, a neighbor let me wait in their apartment until my roommate came home.
   - **Note:** *Neighbor* = a person next door; *neighborhood* = the area (see Test 4).

Test 3 - Social media
1. Which social media websites do you use? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** I mainly use Facebook and Zalo to keep in touch with friends and family, and sometimes LinkedIn for work-related updates.
2. How much time do you spend on social media sites? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** Probably around one or two hours a day in total, mainly because I scroll during breaks and before bed, which I know is not ideal.
3. What kind of information about yourself have you put on social media? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** Mostly photos from trips and simple life updates, because I like sharing happy moments without revealing too much private information.
4. Is there anything you don't like about social media? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** Yes, I dislike fake news and pointless arguments online. Sometimes it feels addictive and wastes a lot of my time.
   - **Note:** *Spend time **on** social media* (preposition *on*).

Test 4 - Your neighborhood
1. Do you like the neighborhood you live in? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** Yes, I like it because it is quite safe and convenient, with supermarkets and cafes within walking distance.
2. What do you do in your neighborhood in your free time? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** I usually go for a walk in the evening or grab coffee at a nearby cafe, mainly because it helps me relax after a long day.
3. What new things would you like to have in your neighborhood? [Why?]
   - **Sample answer:** I would love a small public park or more green space, so residents could exercise and children could play outside safely.
4. Would you like to live in another neighborhood in your town or city? [Why/Why not?]
   - **Sample answer:** Not really, unless I have to move for work. I am used to this area, and moving would be stressful and expensive.
   - **Note:** *Neighborhood* = the area where you live; compare *neighbor* (a person) in Test 2.
