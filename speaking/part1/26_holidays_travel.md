# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Holidays & Travel

## Questions

1. Do you like traveling?
2. Where did you go on your last holiday?
3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?
4. What's your dream travel destination?
5. Do you prefer beach holidays or city trips?
6. How often do you travel?
7. What do you usually do on holidays?
8. Do you think travel is important?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like traveling?
> Yes, I really enjoy traveling. It's a great way to escape the routine of daily life, experience new cultures, and create lasting memories. Even short trips to nearby places can be refreshing.

### Q: Where did you go on your last holiday?
> My last trip was back to my hometown Chau Doc during Tet. I spent time with my family, ate lots of delicious home-cooked food, and visited some local temples. It was a relaxing break from city life.

### Q: Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?
> I prefer traveling with friends or family. Sharing experiences makes the trip more enjoyable, and it's nice to have company for meals and activities. Solo travel can be liberating too, but I find group trips more fun.

### Q: What's your dream travel destination?
> I'd love to visit Japan. I'm fascinated by the mix of traditional culture and cutting-edge technology. The food, the temples, the cherry blossoms—it all seems amazing. I'm also interested in visiting tech hubs to see how the industry operates there.

### Q: Do you prefer beach holidays or city trips?
> It depends on my mood. After busy periods at work, a relaxing beach holiday sounds perfect—just lying on the sand and doing nothing. But usually, I prefer city trips where I can explore, try local food, and see the sights.

### Q: How often do you travel?
> Not as often as I'd like. With work, it's hard to take long vacations. I manage a few short trips each year—usually during Tet to visit family, and maybe one or two trips with friends. I wish I could travel more.

### Q: What do you usually do on holidays?
> I like to explore local attractions, try the regional cuisine, and just relax. I'm not the type to have a packed itinerary—I prefer a balance of sightseeing and downtime. Taking photos and experiencing local culture are important to me.

### Q: Do you think travel is important?
> Absolutely. Travel broadens your horizons and exposes you to different ways of life. It helps you appreciate what you have and makes you more open-minded. Even short trips can provide valuable experiences and perspective.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Escape the routine** - thoát khỏi thói quen hàng ngày
- **Refreshing** - sảng khoái, làm mới tinh thần
- **Liberating** - giải phóng, tự do
- **Cutting-edge** - tiên tiến, hiện đại nhất
- **Tech hub** - trung tâm công nghệ
- **Itinerary** - lịch trình
- **Downtime** - thời gian nghỉ ngơi
- **Broadens your horizons** - mở rộng tầm nhìn
