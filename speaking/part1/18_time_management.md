# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Time Management

## Questions

1. Are you good at managing your time?
2. How do you organize your time?
3. Do you use any tools to help manage your time?
4. Do you think young people manage time well?
5. What do you do when you have free time?
6. Do you ever feel like you don't have enough time?
7. What takes up most of your time?
8. How do you balance work and personal life?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Are you good at managing your time?
> I'd say I'm fairly good at it. As a recruiter, I have to juggle multiple candidates and job openings, so I use calendars and to-do lists to stay organized. But I'll admit I sometimes procrastinate a bit in the evenings when it comes to personal tasks.

### Q: How do you organize your time?
> I use a combination of digital tools and mental planning. I schedule work meetings and deadlines in my calendar app, and I make daily to-do lists to prioritize tasks. For personal activities, I'm more flexible and go with the flow.

### Q: Do you use any tools to help manage your time?
> Yes, I rely heavily on Google Calendar for scheduling and reminder apps for important deadlines. At work, we also use project management tools to track candidate pipelines. These tools are essential for staying on top of everything.

### Q: Do you think young people manage time well?
> It varies from person to person. Some young people are very disciplined and productive, while others struggle with distractions like social media and smartphones. I think schools could do more to teach time management skills.

### Q: What do you do when you have free time?
> I enjoy my hobbies—gaming, watching movies or series, listening to music, exercising, and reading. On weekends, I might meet friends for meals or explore new cafes in the city. Free time is precious, so I try to make the most of it.

### Q: Do you ever feel like you don't have enough time?
> Yes, often. Between work, commuting, exercising, and trying to maintain a social life, days can feel very short. Sometimes I wish there were more hours in a day, but I try to prioritize what's most important.

### Q: What takes up most of your time?
> Work, definitely. I spend most of my day recruiting, interviewing, and coordinating with clients. After that, commuting in Ho Chi Minh City traffic also takes a significant chunk of time each day.

### Q: How do you balance work and personal life?
> It's challenging, but I try to set boundaries. I leave work at a reasonable hour when possible and dedicate evenings and weekends to personal activities and rest. Exercise helps me decompress, and I make sure to schedule time for friends and hobbies.

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Juggle** - xoay sở nhiều việc cùng lúc
- **Procrastinate** - trì hoãn
- **Go with the flow** - tùy cơ ứng biến
- **Pipeline** - quy trình (tuyển dụng)
- **Disciplined** - có kỷ luật
- **Precious** - quý giá
- **Decompress** - giải tỏa căng thẳng
- **Set boundaries** - đặt ra giới hạn
