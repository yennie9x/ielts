# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Gifts

## Questions

1. Do you like giving or receiving gifts?
2. What kind of gifts do you usually give?
3. What was the last gift you received?
4. Do you find it difficult to choose gifts for people?
5. Have you ever received a gift you didn't like?
6. Do you think expensive gifts are better?
7. When do people give gifts in your country?
8. Is gift-giving important in your culture?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like giving or receiving gifts?
> I enjoy both, but I think giving gifts is actually more satisfying. Seeing someone's face light up when they receive something thoughtful brings me a lot of joy. Of course, receiving gifts is nice too—it's always heartwarming to feel remembered.

### Q: What kind of gifts do you usually give?
> I try to give practical and thoughtful gifts based on what the person likes or needs. For tech-savvy friends, it might be gadgets or accessories. For others, it could be books, gift cards to their favorite restaurants, or something related to their hobbies.

### Q: What was the last gift you received?
> My friends gave me a nice pair of headphones for my birthday. It was perfect because they knew I use headphones a lot—for music, gaming, and work calls. I was really happy they picked something so useful.

### Q: Do you find it difficult to choose gifts for people?
> Sometimes, yes. It can be challenging when you don't know what someone wants or they seem to have everything. I usually pay attention to things people mention wanting throughout the year, so I have ideas when gift-giving occasions come up.

### Q: Have you ever received a gift you didn't like?
> Honestly, yes. But I always appreciate the thought behind it. Even if the gift isn't quite my style, I know the person meant well. I'd never show disappointment—I always express gratitude for the gesture.

### Q: Do you think expensive gifts are better?
> Not necessarily. The thought and effort behind a gift matter more than the price tag. A handmade or personalized gift can mean more than something expensive but generic. The best gifts show that the giver understands and cares about the recipient.

### Q: When do people give gifts in your country?
> The biggest gift-giving occasion is Tet, the Vietnamese New Year. People also give gifts for birthdays, weddings, graduations, and when visiting someone's home for the first time. At work, it's common to exchange gifts during Tet with colleagues and clients.

### Q: Is gift-giving important in your culture?
> Yes, it's an important social custom in Vietnam. Gifts symbolize respect, gratitude, and good wishes. During Tet, giving gifts to elders, teachers, and business partners is a way of showing appreciation. The presentation of the gift—nice wrapping, both hands—is also important.

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Thoughtful** - chu đáo, có suy nghĩ
- **Face light up** - mặt rạng rỡ lên
- **Heartwarming** - ấm áp, cảm động
- **Tech-savvy** - am hiểu công nghệ
- **Personalized** - được cá nhân hóa
- **Generic** - chung chung, không đặc biệt
- **Recipient** - người nhận
- **Social custom** - phong tục xã hội
