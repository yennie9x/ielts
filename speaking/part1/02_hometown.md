# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Hometown

## Questions

1. Where are you from?
2. What's your hometown like?
3. Do you like your hometown?
4. How often do you go back to your hometown?
5. What do you miss most about your hometown?
6. What's the difference between your hometown and where you live now?
7. Do you think you'll go back to your hometown in the future?
8. Has your hometown changed much since you were a child?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Where are you from?
> I'm originally from Chau Doc, a small town in the Mekong Delta region, near the Cambodian border. However, I moved to Ho Chi Minh City for work, and I've been living here for a few years now.

### Q: What's your hometown like?
> Chau Doc is a peaceful town known for its rivers, floating markets, and religious sites. It's quite different from Ho Chi Minh City—much quieter and slower-paced. The town is famous for the Sam Mountain and the Tra Su Melaleuca Forest. Many tourists also visit the floating villages there.

### Q: Do you like your hometown?
> Yes, I have a lot of fond memories there. It's where I grew up, and I love the fresh air and delicious local food, especially the fish dishes—Chau Doc is famous for its fish farming. But I moved to Ho Chi Minh City because there are more career opportunities here, especially in the tech industry.

### Q: How often do you go back to your hometown?
> I try to visit during major holidays like Tet, the Vietnamese New Year. It's about a 5 to 6 hour drive from Ho Chi Minh City, so I can't go back as often as I'd like. But when I do, I enjoy spending time with my family and eating my mom's home-cooked meals.

### Q: What do you miss most about your hometown?
> I miss the peaceful atmosphere and being close to nature. Life in Ho Chi Minh City is exciting, but it can be stressful sometimes. In Chau Doc, everything moves at a slower pace, and I find that very relaxing. I also miss the local street food—it's hard to find authentic Chau Doc dishes in the city.

### Q: What's the difference between your hometown and where you live now?
> They're completely different. Chau Doc is a small, quiet town surrounded by rivers and mountains, while Ho Chi Minh City is a bustling metropolis with millions of people. In Chau Doc, people know their neighbors and life is simple. In Ho Chi Minh City, everything is fast-paced—more job opportunities, more entertainment, but also more traffic and pollution.

### Q: Do you think you'll go back to your hometown in the future?
> Maybe when I retire. Right now, my career is in Ho Chi Minh City, and I enjoy the lifestyle here. But I can imagine returning to Chau Doc one day for a quieter life, especially to be closer to my family.

### Q: Has your hometown changed much since you were a child?
> Yes, it has developed quite a bit. There are more modern buildings, better roads, and more tourists visiting. But the core character of the town remains the same—it's still a peaceful place with friendly people and beautiful natural scenery.

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Mekong Delta** - Đồng bằng sông Cửu Long
- **Floating market** - chợ nổi
- **Slower-paced** - nhịp sống chậm hơn
- **Fond memories** - kỷ niệm đẹp
- **Bustling metropolis** - đô thị nhộn nhịp
- **Career opportunities** - cơ hội nghề nghiệp
- **Authentic** - chính gốc, đích thực
