# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Clothes & Fashion

## Questions

1. What kind of clothes do you like to wear?
2. Do you spend a lot of money on clothes?
3. Do you prefer comfortable or fashionable clothes?
4. Do you follow fashion trends?
5. Has your style changed since you were younger?
6. Do you wear different clothes at work and at home?
7. Where do you usually buy your clothes?
8. What's your favorite color to wear?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: What kind of clothes do you like to wear?
> I prefer smart casual clothes—comfortable but still presentable. For work, I usually wear polo shirts or button-up shirts with trousers. At home, I'm much more relaxed in t-shirts and shorts.

### Q: Do you spend a lot of money on clothes?
> Not really. I'm not a big fashion spender. I prefer to buy good quality basics that last longer rather than lots of trendy items. I'd rather invest my money in electronics or experiences.

### Q: Do you prefer comfortable or fashionable clothes?
> Comfort definitely comes first for me. Living in Ho Chi Minh City where it's hot and humid most of the year, I need breathable, comfortable clothes. But I do try to look neat and presentable, especially for work.

### Q: Do you follow fashion trends?
> Not closely. I'm aware of general trends, but I don't actively try to keep up with the latest fashion. I stick to classic styles that don't go out of fashion quickly—it's more practical and economical.

### Q: Has your style changed since you were younger?
> Yes, quite a bit. As a teenager, I wore more casual and sporty clothes. Now, as a working professional, I dress more maturely and pay more attention to looking put-together, especially for work meetings.

### Q: Do you wear different clothes at work and at home?
> Absolutely. At work, I dress more formally—smart casual at minimum. The moment I get home, I change into something comfortable like shorts and a t-shirt. It's like a mental switch to relax mode.

### Q: Where do you usually buy your clothes?
> I buy from both physical stores and online. For basics and casual wear, I often buy online from platforms like Shopee. But for work clothes, I prefer going to stores like Uniqlo where I can try things on.

### Q: What's your favorite color to wear?
> I tend to wear neutral colors—black, white, navy blue, and gray. They're versatile and easy to match. I do occasionally add some color with accessories, but my wardrobe is mostly neutral tones.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Smart casual** - trang phục lịch sự nhưng thoải mái
- **Presentable** - chỉnh tề, đẹp
- **Breathable** - thoáng khí
- **Put-together** - ăn mặc gọn gàng, chỉnh chu
- **Versatile** - linh hoạt, dễ phối
- **Wardrobe** - tủ quần áo / bộ sưu tập quần áo
- **Neutral colors** - màu trung tính
