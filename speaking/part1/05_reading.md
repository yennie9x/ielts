# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Reading

## Questions

1. Do you like reading?
2. What kind of books do you like?
3. How often do you read?
4. Did you read a lot when you were a child?
5. Do you prefer reading physical books or e-books?
6. Where do you usually read?
7. What was the last book you read?
8. Do you think reading is important?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like reading?
> Yes, I enjoy reading, though I don't have as much time as I'd like. I mostly read articles about technology trends and recruitment strategies for work, but I also enjoy novels when I have free time on weekends.

### Q: What kind of books do you like?
> I enjoy a mix of genres. For leisure, I like thrillers and science fiction. For professional development, I read books about business, technology, and human resources. I also occasionally read self-help books.

### Q: How often do you read?
> I read articles online almost daily for work purposes. As for books, I probably finish one or two books a month, usually reading a bit before bed or during weekends.

### Q: Did you read a lot when you were a child?
> Yes, I was quite a bookworm as a kid. I loved adventure stories and comics. My parents encouraged reading, so I spent a lot of time at the local library in Chau Doc.

### Q: Do you prefer reading physical books or e-books?
> It depends on the situation. I prefer e-books for convenience—I can read on my phone during commutes or while waiting. But for leisure reading at home, I still enjoy the feel of a physical book.

### Q: Where do you usually read?
> Mostly at home, in my living room or bedroom. Sometimes I read at cafes on weekends. It's nice to have a coffee while diving into a good book.

### Q: What was the last book you read?
> The last book I finished was about effective hiring practices in the tech industry. It was quite useful for my job. Before that, I read a thriller novel to relax.

### Q: Do you think reading is important?
> Absolutely. Reading expands your knowledge, improves vocabulary, and helps with critical thinking. For my job, staying updated through reading is essential because the tech industry changes so quickly.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Bookworm** - mọt sách
- **Thriller** - truyện/phim ly kỳ
- **Science fiction** - khoa học viễn tưởng
- **Self-help books** - sách phát triển bản thân
- **Commute** - di chuyển đi làm
- **Critical thinking** - tư duy phản biện
- **Leisure** - giải trí, thư giãn
