# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Home / Accommodation

## Questions

1. Do you live in a house or an apartment?
2. What's your favorite room in your home?
3. How long have you lived there?
4. What do you like about your home?
5. Is there anything you would like to change about your home?
6. Do you plan to live there for a long time?
7. What can you see from your window?
8. Who do you live with?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you live in a house or an apartment?
> I live in an apartment in Ho Chi Minh City. It's more practical for city living because apartments are usually closer to work and have good security. Houses in the city center are quite expensive, so apartments are a popular choice for young professionals like me.

### Q: What's your favorite room in your home?
> Definitely my living room. That's where I relax after work—I watch movies, play video games, and sometimes read there. It has a comfortable sofa and good lighting, which makes it perfect for unwinding in the evening.

### Q: How long have you lived there?
> I've been living in my current apartment for about two years now. Before that, I stayed in a smaller place when I first moved to Ho Chi Minh City from Chau Doc.

### Q: What do you like about your home?
> I like that it's conveniently located—close to my workplace and near many restaurants and shops. The apartment is also quite modern with good facilities like a gym and parking area.

### Q: Is there anything you would like to change about your home?
> I wish it had a balcony with a better view. Right now, my window faces another building, so I don't get much natural scenery. A bigger kitchen would also be nice since I occasionally enjoy cooking.

### Q: Do you plan to live there for a long time?
> Probably for another few years. Eventually, I'd like to move to a bigger apartment or maybe even buy my own place. But for now, it suits my needs perfectly.

### Q: What can you see from your window?
> Unfortunately, just another apartment building. The view isn't great, but that's typical for city apartments. Sometimes I can see the city lights at night, which is kind of nice.

### Q: Who do you live with?
> I live alone at the moment. It gives me the freedom to manage my own space and time, which I appreciate since I'm an evening person and sometimes stay up late gaming or watching movies.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Practical** - thiết thực
- **Security** - an ninh
- **Young professional** - người trẻ đi làm
- **Unwinding** - thư giãn
- **Conveniently located** - vị trí thuận tiện
- **Facilities** - tiện ích
- **Balcony** - ban công
