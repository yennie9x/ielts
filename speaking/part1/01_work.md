# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Work

## Questions

1. What do you do?
2. Do you enjoy your job?
3. Why did you choose this job?
4. What are your responsibilities at work?
5. Do you prefer working alone or with others?
6. What do you like most about your job?
7. Is there anything you dislike about your job?
8. Do you think you will change your job in the future?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: What do you do?
> I work as an IT recruiter in Ho Chi Minh City. My job involves finding and interviewing candidates for programming positions at tech companies. I really enjoy it because I get to meet talented people and help them find their dream jobs.

### Q: Do you enjoy your job?
> Yes, I do. It's quite challenging because the tech industry is very competitive, and good developers are in high demand. But it's rewarding when I successfully match a candidate with the right company.

### Q: Why did you choose this job?
> I've always been interested in technology, and I enjoy communicating with people. Being an IT recruiter allows me to combine both interests. I get to learn about the latest tech trends while helping professionals advance their careers.

### Q: What are your responsibilities at work?
> My main responsibilities include sourcing candidates through job portals and LinkedIn, screening resumes, conducting initial interviews, and coordinating with hiring managers. I also need to stay updated on market trends and salary benchmarks in the tech industry.

### Q: Do you prefer working alone or with others?
> I enjoy both, actually. Some tasks like reviewing CVs require focus, so I prefer doing them alone. But I also enjoy collaborating with my team during brainstorming sessions and working closely with hiring managers.

### Q: What do you like most about your job?
> The most satisfying part is when a candidate I placed tells me they're happy with their new job. It feels great to know I played a role in someone's career success.

### Q: Is there anything you dislike about your job?
> Sometimes it can be stressful when there's pressure to fill positions quickly, or when a candidate rejects an offer at the last minute. But overall, the positives outweigh the negatives.

### Q: Do you think you will change your job in the future?
> I might move into a more senior role, like a recruitment manager or HR director. But I'll probably stay in the tech recruitment field because I genuinely enjoy it.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Recruiter** - người tuyển dụng
- **Candidate** - ứng viên
- **Job portal** - trang web tuyển dụng
- **Screen resumes** - sàng lọc hồ sơ
- **Hiring manager** - quản lý tuyển dụng
- **Competitive** - cạnh tranh
- **Rewarding** - bổ ích, đáng giá
