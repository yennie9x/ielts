# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Technology & Phones

## Questions

1. How often do you use your phone?
2. What do you use your phone for?
3. What apps do you use the most?
4. Do you think you spend too much time on your phone?
5. What was your first phone like?
6. How has technology changed your life?
7. Do you prefer calling or texting?
8. Can you live without your phone?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: How often do you use your phone?
> Constantly, to be honest. As a recruiter, I need to be available for candidates and clients. I use it for calls, emails, LinkedIn, and messaging apps throughout the day. It's basically an extension of my work.

### Q: What do you use your phone for?
> Mainly for work—checking emails, browsing LinkedIn, and communicating with candidates. For personal use, I listen to music on Spotify, watch videos on YouTube, play some mobile games, and stay connected with friends through messaging apps.

### Q: What apps do you use the most?
> Probably LinkedIn—it's essential for my job as an IT recruiter. I also use Zalo and Facebook Messenger to communicate with candidates in Vietnam. For personal use, Spotify for music and YouTube for videos are my go-to apps.

### Q: Do you think you spend too much time on your phone?
> Sometimes, yes. It's easy to get sucked into social media or endless scrolling. I try to be more mindful about my screen time, but it's challenging when your work also requires constant phone use.

### Q: What was your first phone like?
> My first phone was a basic Nokia—the kind with physical buttons and a small screen. It could only make calls and send texts, maybe play Snake. It's amazing how much smartphones have evolved since then.

### Q: How has technology changed your life?
> Tremendously. Technology has made my job possible—I can find and contact candidates from anywhere. It's also changed how I consume entertainment, stay informed, and connect with family back in Chau Doc.

### Q: Do you prefer calling or texting?
> For work, I often need to call because it's more personal and efficient for interviews and negotiations. For personal communication, I prefer texting or voice messages—it's less intrusive and I can respond when convenient.

### Q: Can you live without your phone?
> Honestly, it would be very difficult. My job relies heavily on being reachable and accessing information on the go. Personally, I've become so used to having everything at my fingertips. Maybe for a short vacation I could manage, but not long-term.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Extension of** - sự mở rộng của
- **Go-to app** - ứng dụng thường dùng
- **Get sucked into** - bị cuốn vào
- **Mindful** - có ý thức, chú tâm
- **Screen time** - thời gian sử dụng màn hình
- **Intrusive** - làm phiền
- **At your fingertips** - trong tầm tay
