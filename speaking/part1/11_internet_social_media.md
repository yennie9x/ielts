# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Internet & Social Media

## Questions

1. How much time do you spend online?
2. What do you usually do on the internet?
3. Do you use social media?
4. Which social media platforms do you use?
5. Do you think social media is good or bad?
6. How has the internet changed the way people communicate?
7. Do you shop online?
8. Do you think children should use social media?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: How much time do you spend online?
> Quite a lot, honestly. For work, I'm online most of the day—searching for candidates, posting jobs, and communicating via email. In my personal time, I probably spend another 2-3 hours browsing, streaming, or gaming.

### Q: What do you usually do on the internet?
> For work, I use job portals, LinkedIn, and email constantly. For leisure, I stream music and videos, read news and tech articles, play online games, and sometimes shop online.

### Q: Do you use social media?
> Yes, but mainly for professional purposes. LinkedIn is essential for my job. I also use Facebook to keep in touch with family and friends, especially those back in Chau Doc.

### Q: Which social media platforms do you use?
> LinkedIn is my most-used platform for work. I also have Facebook for personal connections and sometimes browse YouTube, though I'm not very active on Instagram or TikTok.

### Q: Do you think social media is good or bad?
> It's a double-edged sword. Social media is great for staying connected, networking, and accessing information. However, it can also be addictive and sometimes spreads misinformation. I think it's about using it responsibly.

### Q: How has the internet changed the way people communicate?
> Dramatically. We can now instantly connect with anyone worldwide. For my job, I can interview candidates remotely through video calls. But I think face-to-face communication has declined, which isn't always positive.

### Q: Do you shop online?
> Yes, frequently. It's convenient, especially for electronics and books. Living in Ho Chi Minh City, delivery is usually fast. I still prefer going to physical stores for clothes though—I like to try things on.

### Q: Do you think children should use social media?
> I think there should be age limits and parental supervision. Social media can expose children to inappropriate content and cyberbullying. But with proper guidance, it can also be educational and help them stay connected with friends.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Job portal** - trang tuyển dụng
- **Leisure** - giải trí
- **Double-edged sword** - con dao hai lưỡi
- **Misinformation** - thông tin sai lệch
- **Responsibly** - có trách nhiệm
- **Cyberbullying** - bắt nạt qua mạng
- **Parental supervision** - sự giám sát của phụ huynh
