# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Birthdays

## Questions

1. How do you usually celebrate your birthday?
2. Do you think birthdays are important?
3. What did you do on your last birthday?
4. Do you prefer big parties or small gatherings?
5. How do people in your country celebrate birthdays?
6. What's the best birthday gift you've received?
7. Do you remember your childhood birthdays?
8. Do you think adults should celebrate birthdays?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: How do you usually celebrate your birthday?
> I keep it relatively simple. I usually have dinner with close friends or family, maybe at a nice restaurant. I'm not big on throwing large parties—I prefer intimate gatherings with people I care about.

### Q: Do you think birthdays are important?
> Yes, I think they're a good opportunity to reflect on the past year and appreciate the people around you. It's also nice to feel celebrated at least once a year. But I don't think they need to be extravagant.

### Q: What did you do on your last birthday?
> I had dinner with a few close friends at a Vietnamese restaurant in Ho Chi Minh City. They surprised me with a cake, which was sweet. I also called my family in Chau Doc to thank them for their wishes.

### Q: Do you prefer big parties or small gatherings?
> Definitely small gatherings. I find large parties overwhelming and hard to have meaningful conversations. With a small group, I can actually spend quality time with each person and enjoy their company.

### Q: How do people in your country celebrate birthdays?
> It varies. For children, families often throw parties with cakes and decorations. Adults tend to have dinner with friends or family. In Vietnam, it's also common for the birthday person to treat others to a meal, rather than receiving gifts—though this is changing with younger generations.

### Q: What's the best birthday gift you've received?
> One year, my friends pitched in to buy me a new pair of headphones I'd been wanting. I was really touched that they remembered and coordinated to get something I actually needed. Practical gifts that show thoughtfulness mean a lot to me.

### Q: Do you remember your childhood birthdays?
> Some of them, yes. I remember birthday parties in Chau Doc with family and neighbors. My mom would make a special meal, and there was always a cake. Those memories are quite precious to me now.

### Q: Do you think adults should celebrate birthdays?
> Why not? Age shouldn't stop you from celebrating. It doesn't have to be extravagant—even a simple dinner with loved ones counts. I think it's healthy to mark another year of life and appreciate what you have.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Intimate gathering** - buổi tụ họp thân mật
- **Extravagant** - xa hoa, hoành tráng
- **Overwhelming** - choáng ngợp
- **Quality time** - thời gian chất lượng
- **Pitched in** - góp tiền/công
- **Thoughtfulness** - sự chu đáo
- **Precious** - quý giá
- **Mark (a milestone)** - đánh dấu (cột mốc)
