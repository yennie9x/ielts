# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Handwriting

## Questions

1. Do you prefer typing or writing by hand?
2. Do you think handwriting is important?
3. How often do you write by hand?
4. Do you have good handwriting?
5. Has your handwriting changed over the years?
6. Do you think children should learn handwriting?
7. When do you write by hand nowadays?
8. Do you think handwriting will become obsolete?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you prefer typing or writing by hand?
> Definitely typing. It's faster and more efficient, especially for work. I spend most of my day on a computer, so typing feels more natural now. But for certain things like quick notes or signing documents, I still write by hand.

### Q: Do you think handwriting is important?
> Yes, to some extent. While typing is more practical for most tasks, handwriting is still a fundamental skill. Studies show it helps with memory and learning. Plus, there's something personal about a handwritten note that typed text can't replicate.

### Q: How often do you write by hand?
> Not very often anymore. I might jot down notes during meetings or write to-do lists on sticky notes. But for emails, reports, and most communication, I type. I probably write by hand only a few times a week.

### Q: Do you have good handwriting?
> It's decent, I'd say—readable but not beautiful. When I was in school, I wrote more neatly because teachers emphasized it. Now that I type most things, my handwriting has gotten a bit messier from lack of practice.

### Q: Has your handwriting changed over the years?
> Yes, it's become less neat since I started working. In school, I wrote more carefully because assignments were handwritten. Now I rarely write by hand, so when I do, it's faster and less tidy than it used to be.

### Q: Do you think children should learn handwriting?
> Yes, I believe it's still an important skill to teach. Even though digital devices are everywhere, handwriting helps with cognitive development and fine motor skills. Children should know how to write before relying entirely on keyboards.

### Q: When do you write by hand nowadays?
> Mainly for quick notes during meetings, signing documents, and sometimes writing birthday cards or personal messages. For anything longer or more formal, I use my computer or phone.

### Q: Do you think handwriting will become obsolete?
> I don't think it will completely disappear, but it will probably become less common. Future generations may write by hand even less than we do. Still, I think there will always be some situations where handwriting is preferred or necessary.

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Efficient** - hiệu quả
- **Fundamental skill** - kỹ năng cơ bản
- **Replicate** - sao chép, tái tạo
- **Jot down** - ghi nhanh
- **Sticky notes** - giấy ghi chú dính
- **Cognitive development** - phát triển nhận thức
- **Fine motor skills** - kỹ năng vận động tinh
- **Obsolete** - lỗi thời
