# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Daily Routine

## Questions

1. What do you usually do in the morning?
2. How do you spend your evenings?
3. What's your typical day like?
4. Do you have the same routine every day?
5. What do you usually do on weekends?
6. Have your daily routines changed recently?
7. What would you like to change about your daily routine?
8. Are you a morning person or an evening person?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: What do you usually do in the morning?
> To be honest, I'm not really a morning person, so I try to keep my mornings simple. I wake up, have a quick breakfast, check my emails, and then ride my motorbike to work. I usually need a cup of coffee to really wake up.

### Q: How do you spend your evenings?
> Evenings are my favorite time. After work, I usually exercise or go to the gym. Then I might play some video games, watch a movie, or listen to music to unwind. I feel more energetic and productive at night.

### Q: What's your typical day like?
> I usually wake up around 7:30, get ready, and head to work by 8:30. I spend most of the day interviewing candidates and coordinating with hiring managers. After work, I hit the gym, have dinner, and then spend a few hours on my hobbies before going to bed around midnight.

### Q: Do you have the same routine every day?
> Weekdays are pretty similar, but weekends are different. On weekends, I sleep in a bit longer and have more time for gaming, meeting friends, or exploring new cafes in the city.

### Q: What do you usually do on weekends?
> I like to catch up on sleep first! Then I might meet up with friends for lunch or dinner. I also spend a good amount of time gaming and watching movies or series. Sometimes I go out to try new restaurants or just relax at home.

### Q: Have your daily routines changed recently?
> Not dramatically, but I've been trying to exercise more regularly. I've also started reading more articles about tech trends to stay updated for my job. These small changes have made me feel more productive.

### Q: What would you like to change about your daily routine?
> I'd like to wake up earlier and become more of a morning person. I've heard that successful people often start their day early, but it's really hard for me since I naturally feel more alert at night.

### Q: Are you a morning person or an evening person?
> Definitely an evening person. I struggle to wake up early, but I come alive at night. That's when I do my best thinking and enjoy my hobbies. I'm most productive after 8 PM.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Morning person** - người thích dậy sớm
- **Evening/Night owl** - người thích thức khuya
- **Unwind** - thư giãn
- **Hit the gym** - đi tập gym
- **Sleep in** - ngủ nướng
- **Catch up on sleep** - ngủ bù
- **Productive** - năng suất
