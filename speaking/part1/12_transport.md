# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Transport

## Questions

1. How do you usually travel to work/school?
2. Do you prefer public or private transport?
3. What's the traffic like in your city?
4. Have you ever used public transport?
5. Do you think public transport should be free?
6. What's your favorite mode of transport?
7. Do you like to travel by plane?
8. How do you think transport will change in the future?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: How do you usually travel to work?
> I ride my motorbike. In Ho Chi Minh City, it's the most convenient way to get around because traffic can be quite heavy, and a motorbike lets me navigate through it more easily than a car would.

### Q: Do you prefer public or private transport?
> Private transport, definitely. The metro system in Ho Chi Minh City is still developing, and buses can be slow due to traffic. My motorbike gives me flexibility and saves time—I can leave whenever I want and take shortcuts.

### Q: What's the traffic like in your city?
> It's notoriously congested, especially during rush hours. Ho Chi Minh City has millions of motorbikes and cars, so traffic jams are a daily reality. It can take twice as long to travel during peak times.

### Q: Have you ever used public transport?
> Yes, occasionally I take buses or the new metro line. It's cheaper and you don't have to worry about parking. But it's not as flexible as having your own vehicle, so I don't use it regularly.

### Q: Do you think public transport should be free?
> That would be ideal, but I'm not sure it's practical. The government would need significant funding to maintain the system. Perhaps subsidized fares would be a good compromise to encourage more people to use public transport.

### Q: What's your favorite mode of transport?
> For daily commuting, my motorbike. It's fast and practical for city life. But for long trips, I enjoy traveling by plane—it's exciting and efficient for covering long distances.

### Q: Do you like to travel by plane?
> Yes, I do. I enjoy the experience of flying, especially the anticipation of arriving at a new destination. It's also the only practical option for traveling far from Ho Chi Minh City, like visiting other countries.

### Q: How do you think transport will change in the future?
> I think we'll see more electric vehicles and improved public transport systems. Ho Chi Minh City is expanding its metro network, which should help reduce traffic. Maybe even autonomous vehicles will become common eventually.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Navigate through** - di chuyển qua
- **Congested** - tắc nghẽn
- **Rush hours** - giờ cao điểm
- **Traffic jam** - kẹt xe
- **Subsidized** - được trợ giá
- **Commuting** - đi lại (đi làm)
- **Autonomous vehicles** - xe tự lái
