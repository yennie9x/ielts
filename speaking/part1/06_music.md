# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Music

## Questions

1. Do you like music?
2. What type of music do you enjoy?
3. Do you play any musical instruments?
4. How often do you listen to music?
5. Do you like to listen to music while working?
6. Have your music preferences changed over the years?
7. Do you prefer listening to music alone or with others?
8. Have you ever been to a concert?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like music?
> Yes, I love music. It's an essential part of my daily life. I listen to music when I'm commuting, working out, relaxing at home, and even sometimes while working.

### Q: What type of music do you enjoy?
> I listen to a variety of genres, but I particularly like pop and electronic music. They're upbeat and help me feel energized. I also enjoy some ballads when I want to relax after a long day.

### Q: Do you play any musical instruments?
> Unfortunately, no. I tried learning guitar when I was younger, but I didn't stick with it. Maybe one day I'll pick it up again when I have more free time.

### Q: How often do you listen to music?
> Almost every day. I use Spotify on my commute to work and during workouts at the gym. Music is like a constant companion for me.

### Q: Do you like to listen to music while working?
> Sometimes, yes. When I'm doing administrative tasks like screening CVs, I put on some background music—usually something instrumental or lo-fi. But when I'm in interviews or calls, I need complete focus, so no music then.

### Q: Have your music preferences changed over the years?
> Definitely. When I was a teenager, I listened to a lot of rock and hip-hop. Now I prefer pop, electronic, and even some classical music for relaxation. I think my taste has become more diverse as I've gotten older.

### Q: Do you prefer listening to music alone or with others?
> Both are enjoyable. I like listening alone when I want to fully immerse myself in the music. But sharing music with friends at a party or gathering is also fun.

### Q: Have you ever been to a concert?
> Yes, a few times. I went to some music festivals and concerts in Ho Chi Minh City. The atmosphere is incredible—there's nothing quite like experiencing live music with thousands of other fans.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Upbeat** - sôi động, vui tươi
- **Ballad** - nhạc trữ tình
- **Commute** - di chuyển (đi làm/về)
- **Lo-fi** - nhạc lo-fi (nhạc nền nhẹ nhàng)
- **Instrumental** - nhạc không lời
- **Diverse** - đa dạng
- **Immerse** - đắm chìm vào
