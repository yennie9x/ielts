# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Video Games / Gaming

## Questions

1. Do you play video games?
2. What kind of games do you like?
3. How often do you play?
4. When did you start playing video games?
5. Do you prefer playing alone or with others?
6. Do you think video games are good or bad for people?
7. What's your favorite game?
8. Do you think adults should play video games?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you play video games?
> Yes, I do! Gaming is one of my favorite hobbies. I mostly play on weekends when I have more free time. It's a great way to de-stress after a busy work week.

### Q: What kind of games do you like?
> I enjoy strategy games and multiplayer online games. I like games that require thinking and planning. Sometimes I also play casual mobile games during short breaks.

### Q: How often do you play?
> During weekdays, maybe an hour or so in the evening if I have time. But on weekends, I can spend several hours gaming, especially if I'm playing with friends online.

### Q: When did you start playing video games?
> I started when I was quite young, probably around 8 or 9 years old. I remember playing simple games at internet cafes in Chau Doc. Gaming has been a hobby of mine ever since.

### Q: Do you prefer playing alone or with others?
> Both have their appeal. Single-player games are great for immersive storytelling. But multiplayer games are more fun because you can team up with friends and compete against others.

### Q: Do you think video games are good or bad for people?
> I think it depends on how you use them. In moderation, games can improve problem-solving skills, reaction time, and even social connections through online play. But excessive gaming can be harmful if it affects your health or responsibilities.

### Q: What's your favorite game?
> Currently, I'm really into strategy games like Age of Empires and some multiplayer battle games. I appreciate games that challenge me mentally and have good replay value.

### Q: Do you think adults should play video games?
> Absolutely! Gaming isn't just for kids. It's a legitimate form of entertainment and stress relief. Many of my colleagues also play games. As long as it doesn't interfere with work or health, I see no problem with adults enjoying video games.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **De-stress** - giải tỏa căng thẳng
- **Strategy games** - game chiến thuật
- **Multiplayer** - nhiều người chơi
- **Immersive** - đắm chìm, nhập vai
- **In moderation** - điều độ
- **Replay value** - giá trị chơi lại
- **Legitimate** - chính đáng, hợp lệ
