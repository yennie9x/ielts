# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Movies & TV

## Questions

1. Do you like watching movies?
2. What kind of films do you like?
3. How often do you watch TV or movies?
4. Do you prefer watching movies at home or in a cinema?
5. Who do you usually watch movies with?
6. What's your favorite movie?
7. Do you watch TV series?
8. Do you think people watch too much TV nowadays?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like watching movies?
> Yes, I love watching movies. It's one of my main ways to relax after work. I enjoy getting lost in a good story, whether it's an action-packed blockbuster or an emotional drama.

### Q: What kind of films do you like?
> I enjoy action movies and thrillers—I love the suspense and excitement. I also like watching tech documentaries and series about startups. Recently, I've been watching a lot of Korean dramas too—they're quite addictive!

### Q: How often do you watch TV or movies?
> Almost every evening. It's my way of relaxing after work. I usually watch one or two episodes of a series before bed, or a full movie on weekends.

### Q: Do you prefer watching movies at home or in a cinema?
> It depends on the movie. For big blockbusters with amazing visuals, I enjoy the cinema experience—the big screen and surround sound make it special. But for most films, I prefer the comfort of watching at home where I can pause and snack freely.

### Q: Who do you usually watch movies with?
> On weekdays, I usually watch alone since it's part of my evening routine. But on weekends, I sometimes watch with friends or go to the cinema with them.

### Q: What's your favorite movie?
> That's a tough question. I really enjoyed "Inception" because it's intellectually stimulating and visually stunning. I've watched it multiple times and notice new details each time.

### Q: Do you watch TV series?
> Yes, quite a lot actually. I'm currently following several series on Netflix. I find series engaging because you can really get invested in the characters over multiple episodes.

### Q: Do you think people watch too much TV nowadays?
> Possibly, yes. With streaming services, it's easy to binge-watch for hours. I try to balance my screen time with other activities like exercise and reading, but I'll admit it's sometimes hard to stop watching a good series.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Blockbuster** - phim bom tấn
- **Suspense** - hồi hộp
- **Addictive** - gây nghiện
- **Surround sound** - âm thanh vòm
- **Intellectually stimulating** - kích thích trí tuệ
- **Binge-watch** - xem liền tù tì nhiều tập
- **Get invested in** - đầu tư cảm xúc vào
