# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Shopping

## Questions

1. Do you enjoy shopping?
2. How often do you go shopping?
3. Do you prefer shopping online or in stores?
4. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?
5. Do you ever buy things you don't need?
6. Have your shopping habits changed over the years?
7. Do you like shopping alone or with others?
8. What's the best place to shop in your city?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you enjoy shopping?
> It depends on what I'm shopping for. I enjoy browsing for electronics and gadgets, or looking at new clothes. But I don't like spending hours in crowded malls—I prefer to know what I need and get it quickly.

### Q: How often do you go shopping?
> For groceries and daily necessities, probably once a week. For other things like clothes or electronics, maybe once a month or less. I'm not a frequent shopper—I usually only buy things when I need them.

### Q: Do you prefer shopping online or in stores?
> For convenience, I often shop online, especially for electronics and books. Delivery in Ho Chi Minh City is usually fast. But for clothes, I prefer going to stores so I can try things on and check the quality in person.

### Q: What do you usually buy when you go shopping?
> Mostly practical things—groceries, household items, and work clothes. Occasionally, I'll treat myself to something fun like a new video game, headphones, or a nice meal at a restaurant.

### Q: Do you ever buy things you don't need?
> Sometimes, yes. Online shopping makes it very easy to impulse buy, especially during sales events. I've bought gadgets that seemed cool but ended up barely using. I try to be more mindful now before clicking "purchase."

### Q: Have your shopping habits changed over the years?
> Definitely. I shop online much more than before—it's just so convenient. I've also become more conscious about quality over quantity. I'd rather buy fewer things that last longer than lots of cheap items.

### Q: Do you like shopping alone or with others?
> Usually alone, because I can move at my own pace and make decisions quickly. But shopping with friends can be fun when we're exploring new stores or helping each other choose clothes.

### Q: What's the best place to shop in your city?
> Ho Chi Minh City has many options. For malls, Vincom Center and AEON are popular. For more unique finds, Nguyen Hue walking street and Ben Thanh Market are interesting. Online platforms like Shopee and Lazada are also very popular here.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Browsing** - xem qua, dạo chơi (mua sắm)
- **Necessities** - nhu yếu phẩm
- **Treat myself** - tự thưởng cho mình
- **Impulse buy** - mua sắm bốc đồng
- **Mindful** - có ý thức
- **Quality over quantity** - chất lượng hơn số lượng
- **At my own pace** - theo tốc độ của mình
