# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Friends

## Questions

1. Do you have many friends?
2. How often do you see your friends?
3. What do you usually do with your friends?
4. How did you meet your best friend?
5. Do you prefer having many friends or a few close friends?
6. Have you made any new friends recently?
7. Do you think friendships change as you get older?
8. What qualities do you value in a friend?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you have many friends?
> I have a moderate number of friends. I have several close friends from university and childhood, and I've also made some new friends through work. I prefer quality over quantity when it comes to friendships.

### Q: How often do you see your friends?
> I try to meet up with friends on weekends. During the week, I'm usually busy with work. We often go out for dinner, watch movies, or just hang out at a café. We also stay connected through group chats.

### Q: What do you usually do with your friends?
> We enjoy having meals together, going to new restaurants, or grabbing coffee. Sometimes we play sports like badminton, or have gaming sessions online. For special occasions, we might go on short trips together.

### Q: How did you meet your best friend?
> I met my best friend during university in Ho Chi Minh City. We were in the same department and worked on group projects together. We discovered we had similar interests and have stayed close ever since.

### Q: Do you prefer having many friends or a few close friends?
> Definitely a few close friends. I value deep, meaningful connections over superficial ones. Close friends are the ones who truly understand you and support you through difficult times.

### Q: Have you made any new friends recently?
> Yes, I've made some friends through my job. As a recruiter, I meet many people, and some have become friends beyond just professional contacts. I've also connected with people through gaming communities online.

### Q: Do you think friendships change as you get older?
> Yes, I believe so. When you're younger, it's easier to make friends because you have more free time and shared environments like school. As an adult, it takes more effort, but the friendships tend to be more mature and stable.

### Q: What qualities do you value in a friend?
> Honesty and loyalty are the most important to me. I appreciate friends who are genuine, supportive, and reliable. A good sense of humor also helps—I like friends who can make me laugh and don't take life too seriously.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Quality over quantity** - chất lượng hơn số lượng
- **Hang out** - đi chơi, la cà
- **Group projects** - dự án nhóm
- **Meaningful connections** - mối quan hệ có ý nghĩa
- **Superficial** - hời hợt, nông cạn
- **Genuine** - chân thành
- **Reliable** - đáng tin cậy
