# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Food & Cooking

## Questions

1. Do you like cooking?
2. What's your favorite food?
3. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out?
4. Can you cook?
5. What food is popular in your country?
6. Have you ever tried foreign food?
7. Do you think it's important to have meals with family?
8. Is there any food you don't like?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like cooking?
> I enjoy it occasionally, but I wouldn't say I'm passionate about it. Living alone, I sometimes cook simple meals to save money and eat healthier. But after a long day at work, I often prefer ordering food or eating out.

### Q: What's your favorite food?
> I love Vietnamese cuisine, especially dishes from my hometown Chau Doc like cá lóc nướng trui (grilled snakehead fish) and bún cá (fish noodle soup). The fish dishes there are exceptional because Chau Doc is famous for fish farming.

### Q: Do you prefer eating at home or eating out?
> It depends on the situation. During weekdays, I often eat out or order delivery because it's convenient. On weekends, I might cook at home or go to nice restaurants with friends to try new places.

### Q: Can you cook?
> Yes, but nothing too fancy. I can make basic Vietnamese dishes like phở, fried rice, and some stir-fry dishes. My mom taught me some recipes from Chau Doc, which I try to recreate when I miss home.

### Q: What food is popular in your country?
> Phở is probably the most famous Vietnamese dish internationally. But we have so many regional specialties—bánh mì, bún chả, cơm tấm, and countless others. Vietnamese food is known for being fresh, flavorful, and using lots of herbs.

### Q: Have you ever tried foreign food?
> Yes, living in Ho Chi Minh City, I have access to many international cuisines. I enjoy Japanese food, especially sushi and ramen. Korean BBQ is also popular here, and I like Italian pasta dishes too.

### Q: Do you think it's important to have meals with family?
> Absolutely. Family meals are a time to bond and share stories. When I visit Chau Doc, eating together with my family is one of the things I look forward to most. It's a Vietnamese tradition that I value.

### Q: Is there any food you don't like?
> I'm not a big fan of very spicy food—my tolerance is quite low. I also don't really enjoy bitter melon, though I know it's healthy. Otherwise, I'm pretty open to trying most foods.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Cuisine** - ẩm thực
- **Regional specialties** - đặc sản vùng miền
- **Flavorful** - đậm đà hương vị
- **Bond** - gắn kết
- **Tolerance** - khả năng chịu đựng
- **Stir-fry** - xào
- **Herbs** - rau thơm
