# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Weather

## Questions

1. What's the weather like in your city?
2. What's your favorite season?
3. Does the weather affect your mood?
4. Do you prefer hot or cold weather?
5. What do you usually do on rainy days?
6. Has the weather in your country changed over the years?
7. Do you check the weather forecast?
8. What's the best weather for outdoor activities?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: What's the weather like in your city?
> Ho Chi Minh City has a tropical climate, so it's hot and humid most of the year. We have two main seasons—the dry season and the rainy season. During the rainy season, from May to November, there are sudden heavy showers almost every afternoon.

### Q: What's your favorite season?
> I prefer the dry season, from December to April. The weather is a bit cooler and more comfortable, and I don't have to worry about getting caught in the rain on my motorbike. It's also better for outdoor activities.

### Q: Does the weather affect your mood?
> Yes, definitely. On sunny days, I feel more energetic and motivated. But during the rainy season, the gloomy weather sometimes makes me feel a bit lazy, and I just want to stay home and watch movies.

### Q: Do you prefer hot or cold weather?
> I'm used to hot weather since I grew up in Vietnam, but I actually prefer slightly cooler weather. When I visit places with temperatures around 20-25°C, I find it very comfortable. The extreme heat in Ho Chi Minh City can be exhausting.

### Q: What do you usually do on rainy days?
> I stay indoors as much as possible. It's the perfect time to relax at home—I might play video games, watch a series, or read. If I have to go out, I wait for the rain to ease before riding my motorbike.

### Q: Has the weather in your country changed over the years?
> I think so. The rainy season seems to have become more intense, with heavier downpours and more flooding in cities. Climate change is probably affecting weather patterns here, like everywhere else.

### Q: Do you check the weather forecast?
> Yes, especially during the rainy season. I check my phone's weather app before leaving for work to see if I need to bring a rain jacket or leave earlier to avoid storms.

### Q: What's the best weather for outdoor activities?
> I'd say around 25-28°C with some cloud cover—not too hot and not raining. Early mornings during the dry season are perfect for exercising outdoors or going to the park.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Tropical climate** - khí hậu nhiệt đới
- **Humid** - ẩm ướt
- **Dry season** - mùa khô
- **Rainy season** - mùa mưa
- **Gloomy** - u ám
- **Downpour** - mưa lớn
- **Flooding** - ngập lụt
- **Cloud cover** - mây che phủ
