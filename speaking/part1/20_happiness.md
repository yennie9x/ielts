# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Happiness

## Questions

1. What makes you happy?
2. Are you a happy person?
3. Do you think money can buy happiness?
4. What do you do when you feel unhappy?
5. Is happiness important to you?
6. Do you think people today are happier than in the past?
7. What's the happiest moment you remember?
8. Can you tell if someone is happy?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: What makes you happy?
> Simple things make me happy—successfully placing a candidate in their dream job, spending time with friends, gaming on weekends, or just relaxing at home watching a good movie. I also feel happy when I discover new music I like or when I visit my family in Chau Doc.

### Q: Are you a happy person?
> I'd say yes, generally. Of course, like everyone, I have stressful days, especially at work. But overall, I'm content with my life—I have a job I enjoy, good friends, hobbies I love, and a family that supports me.

### Q: Do you think money can buy happiness?
> To some extent. Money can provide comfort, security, and access to experiences that bring joy. But beyond a certain point, more money doesn't necessarily mean more happiness. Things like relationships, health, and purpose matter more in the long run.

### Q: What do you do when you feel unhappy?
> I try to do activities that lift my mood—exercising, listening to upbeat music, gaming, or talking to friends. Sometimes I just need time alone to process my feelings. If something at work is bothering me, I try to address it directly rather than letting it fester.

### Q: Is happiness important to you?
> Absolutely. I believe happiness should be a priority in life. That's one reason I chose a career I find meaningful and make time for hobbies I enjoy. Life is too short to be constantly unhappy.

### Q: Do you think people today are happier than in the past?
> That's hard to say. We have more conveniences and opportunities now, but we also face new pressures like social media comparison and work-life imbalance. I think happiness depends more on individual mindset than the era you live in.

### Q: What's the happiest moment you remember?
> One of my happiest moments was when I landed my first job in recruitment in Ho Chi Minh City. It felt like all my hard work paid off, and I was excited to start a new chapter of my life. Family gatherings during Tet in Chau Doc also bring very happy memories.

### Q: Can you tell if someone is happy?
> Usually, yes. Happy people tend to smile more, have positive energy, and seem more relaxed. But sometimes people hide their true feelings, so you can't always tell from the surface. Close friends and family can usually sense it better.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Content** - hài lòng
- **To some extent** - ở một mức độ nào đó
- **In the long run** - về lâu dài
- **Lift my mood** - cải thiện tâm trạng
- **Fester** - âm ỉ, nung nấu (cảm xúc tiêu cực)
- **Priority** - ưu tiên
- **Mindset** - tư duy, lối suy nghĩ
