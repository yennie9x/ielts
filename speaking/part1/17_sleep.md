# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Sleep

## Questions

1. How many hours do you sleep every night?
2. Do you think you get enough sleep?
3. Do you take naps during the day?
4. What time do you usually go to bed?
5. Do you have any problems sleeping?
6. What do you do if you can't fall asleep?
7. Do you think sleep is important?
8. Has your sleep pattern changed over the years?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: How many hours do you sleep every night?
> Usually around 6 to 7 hours. I tend to stay up late because I'm more of a night owl—I feel more productive and creative in the evening. I wish I could sleep more, but there never seems to be enough time.

### Q: Do you think you get enough sleep?
> Honestly, probably not. Health experts recommend 7-8 hours, but I often get less. On weekends, I try to catch up on sleep, but I know it's not ideal. I should probably work on improving my sleep habits.

### Q: Do you take naps during the day?
> Rarely, unless I'm really exhausted. I find that napping makes it harder for me to fall asleep at night. During work hours, I might rest my eyes for a few minutes after lunch, but I don't take proper naps.

### Q: What time do you usually go to bed?
> Usually around midnight or even later. Since I'm an evening person, I tend to do my hobbies like gaming, watching movies, or reading late at night. It's hard to go to bed early when I feel most alert at night.

### Q: Do you have any problems sleeping?
> Sometimes, especially if I've been looking at screens too long before bed. The blue light from phones and computers can keep my mind active. I've started trying to reduce screen time before sleeping, but it's a work in progress.

### Q: What do you do if you can't fall asleep?
> I usually listen to some calm music or a podcast. Sometimes I read a book—physical books work better than e-books for this. I try to avoid checking my phone because that usually makes it worse.

### Q: Do you think sleep is important?
> Absolutely. Sleep affects everything—your energy, mood, concentration, and health. When I don't sleep well, I'm less productive at work and more irritable. Good sleep is essential for overall well-being.

### Q: Has your sleep pattern changed over the years?
> Yes, definitely. When I was younger, I could sleep anywhere, anytime. Now, as an adult with work responsibilities, my sleep is more irregular. I've also become more of a night owl compared to when I was a student.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Night owl** - người thích thức khuya
- **Catch up on sleep** - ngủ bù
- **Sleep pattern/habits** - thói quen ngủ
- **Blue light** - ánh sáng xanh (từ màn hình)
- **Work in progress** - đang trong quá trình cải thiện
- **Irritable** - dễ cáu gắt
- **Well-being** - sức khỏe tổng thể
