# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Sports & Exercise

## Questions

1. Do you play any sports?
2. How often do you exercise?
3. What's your favorite sport?
4. Do you prefer watching sports or playing them?
5. Did you play sports when you were a child?
6. Do you think exercise is important?
7. What sports are popular in your country?
8. Would you like to try any new sports?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you play any sports?
> Yes, I try to exercise regularly. I go to the gym a few times a week, and sometimes I play badminton or go swimming. It's important to stay active, especially since my job involves sitting at a desk most of the day.

### Q: How often do you exercise?
> I aim for about 3-4 times a week. Usually, I go to the gym after work for weight training and cardio. On weekends, I might swim or play sports with friends.

### Q: What's your favorite sport?
> I'd say swimming is my favorite. It's a full-body workout and very refreshing, especially in Ho Chi Minh City's hot weather. I also enjoy badminton because it's fun and social.

### Q: Do you prefer watching sports or playing them?
> I prefer playing. While I occasionally watch football matches, especially big tournaments like the World Cup, I find actually participating in sports more enjoyable and rewarding.

### Q: Did you play sports when you were a child?
> Yes, I was quite active as a kid in Chau Doc. I used to swim in the river, play football with neighborhood kids, and ride bicycles everywhere. Life was more outdoorsy back then.

### Q: Do you think exercise is important?
> Absolutely. Regular exercise keeps you healthy, reduces stress, and improves your mood. For someone like me who works in a sedentary job, it's essential to balance it with physical activity.

### Q: What sports are popular in your country?
> Football is definitely the most popular sport in Vietnam. People go crazy during major tournaments. Badminton and swimming are also widely enjoyed, and martial arts like Vovinam have traditional roots here.

### Q: Would you like to try any new sports?
> I'd love to try tennis. It looks challenging and fun. I'm also interested in hiking—there are some beautiful trails in Vietnam that I'd like to explore.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Weight training** - tập tạ
- **Cardio** - bài tập tim mạch
- **Full-body workout** - bài tập toàn thân
- **Refreshing** - sảng khoái
- **Sedentary job** - công việc ngồi nhiều
- **Outdoorsy** - thích hoạt động ngoài trời
- **Martial arts** - võ thuật
