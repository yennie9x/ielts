# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Photos & Photography

## Questions

1. Do you like taking photos?
2. Do you prefer taking photos with a phone or a camera?
3. What kind of photos do you like to take?
4. Do you post photos on social media?
5. Do you like looking at old photos?
6. Do you prefer taking selfies or photos of scenery?
7. Have you ever taken a photography class?
8. Do you think people take too many photos nowadays?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you like taking photos?
> I enjoy it casually, but I'm not particularly passionate about photography. I take photos when I travel, at special events, or when I want to remember a moment with friends. But I'm not the type to photograph everything I see.

### Q: Do you prefer taking photos with a phone or a camera?
> Definitely my phone. It's convenient since I always have it with me, and smartphone cameras are quite good nowadays. I don't own a professional camera—my phone meets all my photography needs.

### Q: What kind of photos do you like to take?
> Mostly photos of food, interesting places, and moments with friends. When I visit my hometown Chau Doc, I like to capture the beautiful scenery—the rivers, Sam Mountain, and the floating markets.

### Q: Do you post photos on social media?
> Occasionally, but not frequently. I might share photos from trips or special occasions on Facebook. I'm not very active on Instagram, so I don't feel the pressure to constantly post photos.

### Q: Do you like looking at old photos?
> Yes, it's quite nostalgic. Looking at old photos reminds me of happy memories—childhood in Chau Doc, university days, gatherings with friends. It's nice to see how much things have changed over the years.

### Q: Do you prefer taking selfies or photos of scenery?
> I prefer photos of scenery and places. I'm a bit camera-shy, so I don't take many selfies. I'd rather capture a beautiful sunset or an interesting street scene than my own face.

### Q: Have you ever taken a photography class?
> No, I've never taken a formal class. I've watched some YouTube tutorials about basic photography techniques, but most of what I know is self-taught through trial and error.

### Q: Do you think people take too many photos nowadays?
> Perhaps. With smartphones, it's easy to photograph everything. Sometimes people are so focused on capturing the moment that they forget to actually experience it. I try to find a balance—take a few photos, then put the phone away and enjoy.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Casually** - một cách thoải mái, không chuyên nghiệp
- **Passionate** - đam mê
- **Nostalgic** - hoài niệm
- **Camera-shy** - ngại chụp ảnh
- **Self-taught** - tự học
- **Trial and error** - thử và sai
- **Find a balance** - tìm sự cân bằng
