# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Colors

## Questions

1. What's your favorite color?
2. Do you like dark or bright colors?
3. Do colors affect your mood?
4. Were your favorite colors different when you were young?
5. What color do you usually wear?
6. Do you think colors are important in everyday life?
7. What colors are popular in your country?
8. Would you ever paint your room a bright color?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: What's your favorite color?
> I'd say blue is my favorite color. It's calming and versatile—it goes well with many other colors. I also like black because it's sleek and professional, which suits my work environment.

### Q: Do you like dark or bright colors?
> I generally prefer darker or neutral colors, especially for clothes. They're more practical and easier to match. But for things like home decor, I appreciate some brighter accents to add warmth and energy.

### Q: Do colors affect your mood?
> Yes, I believe they do. Bright, warm colors like yellow and orange feel energizing, while blue and green feel calming. That's partly why I keep my apartment in neutral tones—it feels more relaxing after a busy day at work.

### Q: Were your favorite colors different when you were young?
> Yes, I think so. As a child, I liked brighter colors like red and green. As I've gotten older, my preferences have shifted to more subdued colors like blue, gray, and black. I think it's a natural part of maturing.

### Q: What color do you usually wear?
> I tend to wear neutral colors—black, white, navy blue, and gray. They're versatile and easy to match, which makes getting dressed in the morning simpler. I don't often wear very bright colors.

### Q: Do you think colors are important in everyday life?
> Definitely. Colors influence our emotions, perceptions, and even decisions. In marketing and branding, colors are carefully chosen to create certain impressions. Even in daily life, the colors around us affect how we feel in a space.

### Q: What colors are popular in your country?
> Red and yellow are significant in Vietnamese culture—they symbolize luck, prosperity, and happiness. You see them everywhere during Tet. For everyday fashion, though, Vietnamese people wear a wide range of colors.

### Q: Would you ever paint your room a bright color?
> Probably not the whole room, but maybe an accent wall. I prefer neutral, calming colors for my living space since I spend evenings relaxing there. Too much bright color might be overwhelming and make it hard to unwind.

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Calming** - êm dịu, thư giãn
- **Versatile** - linh hoạt, đa dụng
- **Sleek** - bóng bẩy, thanh lịch
- **Home decor** - trang trí nhà cửa
- **Accents** - điểm nhấn
- **Subdued colors** - màu sắc nhẹ nhàng, trầm
- **Prosperity** - sự thịnh vượng
- **Accent wall** - bức tường điểm nhấn
