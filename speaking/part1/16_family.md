# IELTS Speaking Part 1: Family

## Questions

1. Do you have a big family?
2. Who are you closest to in your family?
3. Do you spend a lot of time with your family?
4. What do you like to do together as a family?
5. How often do you see your extended family?
6. Are family relationships important in your culture?
7. Do you look like anyone in your family?
8. Would you prefer to live with your family or alone?

---

## Sample Answers

### Q: Do you have a big family?
> I have a medium-sized family. My immediate family includes my parents and one sibling. We also have extended family—grandparents, aunts, uncles, and cousins—most of whom still live in Chau Doc.

### Q: Who are you closest to in your family?
> I'm probably closest to my mother. She's always been supportive of my decisions, including my move to Ho Chi Minh City for work. We talk on the phone regularly, and she sends me home-cooked food whenever relatives visit the city.

### Q: Do you spend a lot of time with your family?
> Not as much as I'd like, since I live in Ho Chi Minh City and they're in Chau Doc. But I try to visit during major holidays like Tet, and we stay connected through video calls and messaging throughout the year.

### Q: What do you like to do together as a family?
> When I visit home, we enjoy having big family meals together. My mom cooks traditional Chau Doc dishes, and we sit around talking for hours. We also visit temples and relatives during Tet, which is a special tradition.

### Q: How often do you see your extended family?
> Usually during Tet and other important occasions like weddings or anniversaries. Since most of my extended family is in Chau Doc, the journey takes about 5-6 hours, so I can't visit as frequently as I'd like.

### Q: Are family relationships important in your culture?
> Very much so. In Vietnamese culture, family is central to life. We're taught to respect our elders, maintain close family ties, and support each other. Many families still have multiple generations living together.

### Q: Do you look like anyone in your family?
> People say I look like my father, especially around the eyes and face shape. My personality, though, is more like my mother's—we're both quite calm and patient.

### Q: Would you prefer to live with your family or alone?
> At this stage of my life, I prefer living alone because of work convenience and personal independence. But I miss my family and can imagine living closer to them in the future, perhaps when I'm older or have my own family.

---

## Useful Vocabulary

- **Immediate family** - gia đình ruột
- **Extended family** - họ hàng
- **Supportive** - ủng hộ
- **Traditions** - truyền thống
- **Respect elders** - kính trọng người lớn tuổi
- **Multiple generations** - nhiều thế hệ
- **Independence** - sự độc lập
