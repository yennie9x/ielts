## Why is the birth rate falling in many parts of the world?

**Type:** Writing · reasons (2 ideas)  
**Shape:** intro → money + example → marry later → end

These days, the **birth rate** is falling in many countries. There are two main reasons.

The first is money. **Raising a child** is very expensive, so many people feel they **cannot afford a family**. For example, a study in England shows that, **on average**, it costs around £224,000 to **bring up a child** from birth. That is why a lot of couples wait, or have only one child.

People also **have a tendency to** finish their education and get a **stable job** before they have children. In the past, getting married was a **cornerstone event**. These days it is more of a **capstone event** — people want a house, **a savings account**, and some experience first. As they **get married later**, they usually have fewer children.

I believe both money and marrying later matter. If life stays this expensive, birth rates will probably **keep falling**.

**Collocation**
- birth rate / fertility rate
- raise a child / bring up a child
- cannot afford a family
- on average
- have a tendency to
- a stable job
- a savings account
- cornerstone event / capstone event
- get married later
- keep falling

**Grammar**
- so ...
- For example, ...
- That is why ...
- while / These days it is more of ...
- As they ..., they ...
- If ..., ... will probably ...

**Dropped (list / template)**  
First of all · there is no doubt · lastly · pushy parents as a 3rd idea (optional extra in the exam, not a new paragraph unless you drop one of the two above)

## Speaking — A skill you want to improve

**Type:** Speaking · Part 2  
**Shape:** what the skill is → why I fail → what I will do

Well, I have to admit — I still need to improve a lot of skills, at work and at home. But **time management** is the one I need to **get better at right away**.

The thing is, I tend to **procrastinate**. I often **wait until the last minute** to finish something, so the work **doesn’t come out well**. A big reason is entertainment. Social media is just so tempting — I keep **scrolling** through videos on Facebook and TikTok **non-stop**, one after another. I also spend hours on computer games.

**Looking back**, I think I need a daily schedule — and I actually have to **stick to it**. So this year, I’m going to **stay focused**, **cut down on screen time**, and follow that plan so I can actually **reach my goals**.

**Collocation**
- get better at (something) right away
- tend to procrastinate
- wait until the last minute
- doesn’t come out well
- keep scrolling
- non-stop
- looking back
- stick to it
- stay focused
- cut down on screen time
- reach my goals

**Grammar**
- I have to admit ...
- the one I need to ...
- so ...
- I keep + -ing
- so I can ...

This is speaking. Do not rewrite it with writing frames (`These days` / `I believe` / `In the end`).
