# Pre-IELTS — Sept 20

## Speaking — A time you changed your opinion

Well, when I was a kid, I thought outstanding people always had a special talent — like a gift they were born with. I wasn’t that smart. I struggled at school and believed I could never keep pace with my friends.

Then I read a book about the growth mindset, and it completely changed my mind. The writer said our character traits can change if we work hard and hone certain skills regularly. That’s when I came to realize successful people aren’t always the smartest — they’re the ones who never give up.

I see this at work. A teammate started below me, even though we’re the same age. He doesn’t compare himself to others, he’s ambitious, and he’s not afraid to ask when he doesn’t know something. He got promoted pretty quickly, and now we’re at the same level.

So I don’t believe talent is everything anymore. Hard work and attitude matter more.
