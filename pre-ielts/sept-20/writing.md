## Part 1 — Why do many companies prefer experienced candidates?

**Discuss both sides:** experienced workers vs new graduates. Give your opinion.

These days, many companies prefer people with work experience, while others are willing to hire new graduates. Both choices have clear benefits.

People with experience can usually start work almost immediately. They need little training, so the company saves time and money. They also handle problems at work better, because they have faced similar situations before. That is why many managers feel safer hiring someone who already knows the job.

New graduates have advantages too. They often accept lower pay, so they cost less. They are young, they welcome new ideas, and many of them are good with technology. The company can also train them in its own way from the start.

I believe experience still matters more for most companies, especially if the job is difficult. But if the role is fairly simple, hiring a graduate can be a good idea, and it costs less.

## Part 2 — What should people do when they feel stressed?

These days, many people feel stressed because of work, family, or money. If this feeling lasts for a long time, it can affect our health. We may lose sleep, lose focus, and find it harder to enjoy daily life. The good news is that there are simple things we can do.

The first step is to find out where the stress comes from. If it comes from work — for example, too many tasks and not enough time — it is better to talk to a manager and ask how to share the work, instead of staying quiet and getting more tired. If it comes from family, sitting down and talking honestly can help everyone understand each other. Asking others for help is useful, but we also need to say clearly what we need. For instance, someone who always brings work home could agree with their boss on a more realistic deadline. Once the real problem is clear, it becomes easier to fix.

Helping ourselves is even more important. We cannot always change our job or our family overnight, but we can look after our body and mind. Taking a few days off, getting enough sleep, and walking in nature can calm us down when our head is full of worries. Playing a sport after work is another simple way to let go of the day’s pressure. These habits do not remove every problem, but they give us enough energy to face the problem again.

In the end, we all get stressed sometimes. If we find the cause, ask for help when we need it, and take care of ourselves, stress can become a chance to slow down, stay calm, and come back stronger.

- Mở: vì sao stress đáng lo + sẽ nêu cách
