IELTS Speaking review — Band 6.5
Goal: sound like a real person. Short, clear stories. Do not sound like writing.

Part 1
[ ] I answer, then add 1 extra sentence (why / example)
[ ] I do not give a 1-word answer
[ ] I do not give a memorised speech

Part 2 (the cue card)
[ ] I cover the points on the card
[ ] Story has a clear shape: past view → what changed → example → what I think now
[ ] I speak for about 1.5–2 minutes
[ ] I use one real example (work / school / family) — not only general ideas
[ ] I do not list the same idea 3–4 times (talent / aptitude / gift = one idea)

Part 3
[ ] I give an opinion + a reason + a short example
[ ] If I need time: "Let me think... / That's an interesting question."
[ ] I compare or talk about "some people / other people" when I can

Natural language (speaking is OK to be spoken)
[ ] Contractions: I don't, he's, that's
[ ] Useful chunks: I used to think / I came to realize / keep pace with / I'm not afraid to ask
[ ] I do NOT use writing phrases: "My contention is that", "It is my firm belief that"
[ ] I fix words I often mix: thought (not though), excellent, ambitious, Taylor Swift / singer
[ ] not afraid to ask (not "do not afraid"); doesn't know something (not "unknow about")
[ ] succeed / be successful (not get success)

Pronunciation (my weak points)
[ ] I finish the last sound: right, wait, of, so
[ ] wait /weɪt/ ≠ waste /weɪst/
[ ] Hard clusters slow then fast: stick to that plan, scrolling, right away
[ ] I pause at the end of an idea, not in the middle of a phrase

After I practice / record
[ ] A listener can follow my story without reading notes
[ ] I did not restart the same sentence twice
[ ] 2–3 new words only — I can say them clearly
