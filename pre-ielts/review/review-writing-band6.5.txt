# IELTS Writing review — Band 6.5

**Goal:** natural, everyday English. Same 4-paragraph shape every time. Change only the topic words.

**Bold** = collocation (Lexical 6.5)

---

## Shape (reuse for any topic)

1. Intro — topic + 2 sides / 2 ways
2. Body 1 — idea + why + result + one example
3. Body 2 — other side or second way + why + result + one example
4. End — my view in 1–2 sentences (no new idea)

### Frames (change only the CAPS)

**Intro — discuss both**  
These days, many people / companies [DO X], while others [DO Y]. Both choices have **clear benefits**.

**Intro — problem–solution**  
These days, many people [HAVE PROBLEM] because of [CAUSE]. If this lasts for a long time, it can **affect** [WHO / WHAT]. The good news is that there are simple things we can do.

**Body — one idea**  
[GROUP] can usually [BENEFIT]. They [WHY], so [RESULT]. That is why [WHO] feel / think [VIEW]. For example, [ONE SHORT EXAMPLE].

**Body — other side**  
[OTHER GROUP] have advantages too. They [BENEFIT], so [RESULT]. They also [SECOND SMALL POINT].

**Body — a solution**  
The first step is to [SOLUTION 1]. If it comes from [CAUSE], we should [ACTION], instead of [BAD ACTION]. For instance, [EXAMPLE]. Once [SMALL RESULT], it becomes easier to [BIG RESULT].

**End — opinion**  
I believe [SIDE A] still **matters more**, especially if [WHEN]. But if [OTHER CASE], [SIDE B] can be **a good idea**.

**End — solutions**  
In the end, we all [PROBLEM] sometimes. If we [WAY 1] and [WAY 2], [PROBLEM] can become easier to handle.

## Paraphrase bank

**Open**
- These days / Today (pick ONE — not nowadays + today's)
- many people
- some ... while others ...
- Both choices have **clear benefits**
- There are simple things we can do

**Next idea**
- ... too / Also
- The first step is to ...
- More importantly
- That is why ...
- But / However

**Result**
- so ...
- As a result, ...
- This means ...
- it becomes easier to ...

**Example**
- For example / For instance
- someone who ... could ...

**View**
- I believe ...
- still matters more
- can be a good idea
- In the end, ...

**Do not use**  
because of the following reasons · First of all · Last but not least · it is without doubt that · Evidence shows that · a growing number of · it is widely believed · furthermore · consequently · ASAP · ppl · newbies · figure out

## What gets the score

You do not need rare words. You need the **right word + partner** and a few longer sentences.

Aim: **6+ collocations** and **3+ grammar patterns** in every essay.

**Collocation**
- prefer people with / willing to hire / have clear benefits
- start work almost immediately / need little training
- save time and money / feel safer / know the job
- accept lower pay / welcome new ideas / good with technology
- matters more / fairly simple / a good idea
- last for a long time / affect our health
- find out where ... comes from / talk to a manager
- a more realistic deadline / look after our body and mind
- take care of ourselves / come back stronger
- unwind after / in small amounts / turn into a real problem
- struggle to stop / find it hard to focus
- log ... out / in moderation / control our lives

**Grammar**
- while (two sides in one sentence)
- If ..., it can / we should
- so / That is why
- instead of + -ing
- someone who ... / a student who ...
- Once ..., it becomes
- When ..., they ... so ...
- especially if / But if

Do not chase Band 8 grammar. Wrong + fancy = Band 5.

## Sample A — Jobs (discuss both)

**Type:** Writing · discuss both + opinion  
**Shape:** intro → side A + example → side B → end

These days, many companies **prefer people with** work experience, while others are **willing to hire** new graduates. Both choices have **clear benefits**.

People with experience can usually **start work almost immediately**. They **need little training**, so the company can **save time and money**. That is why many managers **feel safer** hiring someone who already **knows the job**.

New graduates have advantages too. They often **accept lower pay**, so they cost less. They **welcome new ideas**, and many of them are **good with technology**.

I believe experience still **matters more**, especially if the job is difficult. But if the role is **fairly simple**, hiring a graduate can be **a good idea**.

**Collocation**
- prefer people with
- willing to hire
- have clear benefits
- start work almost immediately
- need little training
- save time and money
- feel safer / know the job
- accept lower pay
- welcome new ideas
- good with technology
- matters more / fairly simple / a good idea

**Grammar**
- while others ...
- so ...
- That is why ...
- someone who ...
- especially if ...
- But if ...

## Sample B — Stress (problem–solution)

**Type:** Writing · problem–solution  
**Shape:** intro → find the cause + example → help ourselves → end

These days, many people feel stressed because of work, family, or money. If this lasts for a long time, it can **affect our health**. The good news is that there are simple things we can do.

The first step is to **find out where** the stress **comes from**. If it comes from work, we should **talk to a manager**, instead of staying quiet. For instance, someone who always brings work home could agree on **a more realistic deadline**.

Helping ourselves is even more important. We cannot change everything overnight, but we can **look after our body and mind**.

In the end, we all get stressed sometimes. If we find the cause and **take care of ourselves**, it becomes easier to **come back stronger**.

**Collocation**
- affect our health
- find out where ... comes from
- talk to a manager
- a more realistic deadline
- look after our body and mind
- take care of ourselves
- come back stronger

**Grammar**
- If this lasts for a long time, it can ...
- If it comes from ...
- instead of + -ing
- someone who ...
- If we ... it becomes easier to ...

## Sample C — Computer games (problem–solution)

**Type:** Writing · problem–solution  
**Shape:** intro → problem + example → solutions → end

These days, many young people play games to **unwind after** study or work. This is fine **in small amounts**, but it often **turns into a real problem**.

The main problem is overuse. When people play for hours every day, they **struggle to stop**, so they sleep badly and do worse at school. For example, a student who games until late will **find it hard to focus** the next morning.

There are simple things we can do. Apps can **log players out** after a set time. More importantly, people need self-control — about 30 minutes to one hour a day is reasonable.

In the end, playing games is fine if we play **in moderation**. We should not let games **control our lives**.

**Collocation**
- unwind after
- in small amounts
- turn into a real problem
- struggle to stop
- find it hard to focus
- log players out
- in moderation
- control our lives

**Grammar**
- but ...
- When ..., they ... so ...
- a student who ...
- if we ...

## Checklist

**Before**
- [ ] Task type is clear: opinion / discuss both + opinion / problem–solution
- [ ] I used the 4-paragraph shape
- [ ] 2 ideas only + 1 example each
- [ ] 250+ words / about 40 minutes

**Task**
- [ ] Every part of the question is answered
- [ ] Discuss both? BOTH sides + my opinion
- [ ] Each idea has why + result
- [ ] I did not list 5–6 points
- [ ] Conclusion has no new idea

**Language (Lexical 6.5)**
- [ ] I only changed the topic words in the frames
- [ ] I used the paraphrase bank, not template sentences
- [ ] I used 6+ collocations (right word + partner)
- [ ] No slang; no fancy word I cannot reuse
- [ ] I did not repeat the same idea twice

**Grammar (Grammar 6.5)**
- [ ] Subject + verb in every sentence
- [ ] affect / lose (not effect / loose)
- [ ] take care of ourselves; devote ourselves to
- [ ] a priority FOR; nowadays OR today's (not both)
- [ ] I used 3+ patterns: while / If ... / so / That is why / instead of / someone who / Once ... / especially if
- [ ] At least 4 sentences are longer than Subject + Verb + object

**After (1 minute)**
- [ ] I can read it out loud easily
- [ ] I can copy this shape onto a new topic in 5 minutes
