IELTS Listening review — Band 6.5
Goal: catch the change. The first thing I hear is often a trap.

Before each section
[ ] I use the silent time to read ALL questions in that part
[ ] I predict: number? name? place? noun? verb?
[ ] I underline words that may be paraphrased

While listening
[ ] I keep my pen on the next question so I do not get lost
[ ] If I miss one, I skip it and follow the next — I do not freeze
[ ] I listen for corrections: "Tuesday... sorry, Thursday"
[ ] I listen for synonyms of the question words, not only the same word

Answers
[ ] Spelling must be correct (especially names, weeks, common words)
[ ] Plural -s if I hear more than one
[ ] Numbers: 15 / 50, 13 / 30 — I check
[ ] I follow the word limit (ONE WORD / NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS)
[ ] I do not write extra words the gap does not need

Traps I watch for
[ ] They mention two options, then change to the real answer
[ ] Similar names or similar times
[ ] Negatives: not, instead of, rather than

At the end (transfer / check)
[ ] Every question has an answer (guess if empty)
[ ] Capital letters for names / places if needed
[ ] I did not put the right answer on the wrong number

After practice
[ ] I replay only the questions I missed and write WHY (speed / spelling / trap / lost place)
[ ] I add 3 new words I heard to my list
