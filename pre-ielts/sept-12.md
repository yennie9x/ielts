## Topic 1 — Computer games

**Type:** Writing · problem–solution  
**Shape:** intro → problem + example → solutions → end

These days, many young people play computer games to **unwind after** a long day of study or work. This is fine **in small amounts**, but it often **turns into a real problem**. If this lasts for a long time, it can **affect our health** and our school results.

The main problem is overuse. When people play for hours every day, they **struggle to stop**, so they take less exercise, sleep badly, and do worse at school or at work. For example, a student who games until late at night will **find it hard to focus** the next morning. That is why many parents worry.

There are simple things we can do. Apps can **log players out** after a set time, so children cannot play all night. More importantly, people need self-control. Playing for about 30 minutes to one hour a day is reasonable. We should use games to relax, instead of letting them **control our lives**.

In the end, playing games is fine if we play **in moderation**. If we **set a time limit** and **stick to it**, games can help us relax — they do not have to **take over our lives**.

**Collocation**
- unwind after
- in small amounts
- turn into a real problem
- affect our health
- struggle to stop
- find it hard to focus
- log players out
- in moderation
- control our lives / take over our lives
- set a time limit / stick to it

**Grammar**
- If this lasts for a long time, it can ...
- When ..., they ... so ...
- a student who ...
- That is why ...
- instead of + -ing
- If we ...

## Topic 2 — The person I admire

**Type:** Speaking  
**Shape:** who → why I like him → character → example → close

I follow Ha Anh Tuan, a singer who is now in his fifties. He is **well educated** — he studied medicine before he became a singer.

His voice is beautiful and touching. His songs are thoughtful, and they can heal us when we are **going through a hard time**.

He **comes from a wealthy family**, but he does **not take that for granted**. He still works hard and **keeps learning**. Medicine is a difficult major, and after that he **chased his second dream** of becoming a singer.

What I admire most is his **charity work**. He helps children with cancer and **supports elderly people**. I rarely see that in other celebrities. That’s why he’s one of my idols. I still **go back to his songs** when I need comfort, and I always **look forward to** his new music.

**Collocation**
- well educated
- going through a hard time
- come from a wealthy family
- take something for granted
- keep learning
- chase a dream
- charity work
- support elderly people
- go back to his songs
- look forward to his new music

**Grammar**
- a singer who ...
- before he became ...
- when we are ...
- but ...
- What I admire most is ...

Do not say: *he doesn't need to keep learning or take everything for granted* (unclear).
