## Speaking Part 2 & 3 (cue cards / discussion)

| Topic area | Ví dụ đề Part 2 |
|------------|------------------|
| **People** | describe a person you admire, a friend, a teacher, a famous person |
| **Places** | a city, a tourist place, a café, your hometown, a building |
| **Objects** | a gift, something you bought, an old thing, a photo |
| **Events** | a celebration, a trip, a concert, a sports event, a difficult decision |
| **Experiences** | a time you helped someone, learned something, felt proud |
| **Media & technology** | a website, an app, a film, a book, social media |
| **Work & study** | a course, a skill you want to learn, a project, a job |
| **Health & lifestyle** | healthy activity, sport you tried, a habit you want to change |
| **Environment** | pollution, recycling, protecting nature (often Part 3) |
| **Society** | community, volunteering, rules, crime, traffic |

Part 3 thường mở rộng: **advantages/disadvantages, compare past & present, future trends, government role, young vs old**.

## Topic map theo kỹ năng (ôn nhanh)

```
Speaking P1  → cá nhân, thói quen, sở thích (ngắn, 1–2 câu)
Speaking P2  → kể chuyện 1–2 phút (cue card)
Speaking P3  → ý kiến, so sánh, giải thích sâu
Writing T2   → luận 250+ từ (4–5 đoạn)
Reading/Listening → từ vựng học thuật theo lĩnh vực trên
```

---

## Gợi ý luyện theo topic

1. Chọn **1 topic/tuần** (ví dụ: Education).
2. **Speaking:** 4 câu Part 1 + 1 cue card Part 2 + 3 câu Part 3.
3. **Writing:** 1 outline + 1 bài 40 phút (hoặc chỉ intro + 2 body).
4. **Vocab:** 15–20 từ/collocation + 1 note ngữ pháp (như trong `speaking/15.part1.test*.md`).
5. **Listening/Reading:** 1 passage cùng chủ đề (VOA, Cambridge, hoặc `reading/`).
