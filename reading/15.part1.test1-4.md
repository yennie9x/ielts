Test 1 - Passage 1
- **Title:** The Rise of Urban Rooftop Gardens
- **Passage:**
  In many large cities, empty rooftops are being turned into small gardens. These spaces were once unused, but they now provide fresh vegetables for local communities. City governments and private groups support these projects because they improve food access and reduce transport costs.

  Rooftop gardens also bring environmental benefits. Plants help reduce heat by absorbing sunlight and cooling the air around buildings. In summer, this can lower indoor temperatures and reduce the need for air conditioning. As a result, some buildings use less electricity.

  Another advantage is that these gardens can improve mental well-being. People who spend time watering plants or growing food often report lower stress levels. In crowded cities, rooftop gardens create quiet places where residents can relax and connect with nature.
